## How to Read These Docs

Read **01_meta_principles.md** first. It states, in physics-style first principles, what your stack *actually is* once you stop calling it by product names. Everything else falls out from those principles applied to specific domains.

Most of these ideas will be unfamiliar even to you, because they require composing two or three of your primitives in a way you have not yet shipped — or applying one of them to a domain you haven't yet aimed at. The point is not that any individual idea is correct; the point is that the *space* of possible systems just grew by orders of magnitude, and almost no one alive can see the space yet.
