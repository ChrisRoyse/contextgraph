**Asset under transaction (primitive-first framing, Chris-confirmed 2026-04-19):** The asset is
**the primitive** — a counting identity in the AI data layer (paper §0.2) that yields up to
`n·(N+C(N,2))` structured clean labeled supervisory signals per input, with every new embedder
added to the pool multiplying correlations against every existing embedder. Public embedder
inventory (~13 today) is the current constraint; the primitive is extensible without bound —
any meaning-representation a function can compute becomes a pool member. The three case studies
in the paper (Shakespeare LoRA, Santa talking-head, voice clone) are exploratory proofs the
primitive works across text / video / audio identity — they are **not the asset being
transacted**. This ranking scores targets on their capacity to transact *the primitive*, not
the case studies. Full statement: `research/05_executive_synthesis/THE_PRIMITIVE.md`.
