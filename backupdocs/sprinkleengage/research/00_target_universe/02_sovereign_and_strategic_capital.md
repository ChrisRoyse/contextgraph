# Tier C — Sovereign Wealth + Strategic Capital Scan ($3B+ Capable)

## Executive Summary

Tier C is where the $3B+ check question for a **data-layer primitive** is most plausibly
answered **without acquisition**. Unlike Tier A (hyperscaler acqui-hire) or Tier B (frontier-lab
corporate move), Tier C writes **strategic investments, mega-licensing, and sovereign-data
partnerships** at the scale Teleox's primitive (paper §0.2 counting identity + embedder-pool
extensibility — see `research/05_executive_synthesis/THE_PRIMITIVE.md`) requires. **The
sovereign pitch is uniquely primitive-first**: a sovereign can commission embedders for their
own national-specific meaning-representations (dialect, legal taxonomy, cultural entity graphs,
domain sensors) — every custom embedder added to the pool multiplies correlations against every
existing embedder. The case studies (Shakespeare LoRA, Santa talking-head, voice clone) are
exploratory proofs; they are not products being pitched to sovereigns.

The dominant finding: **the Gulf sovereign stack (MGX + G42 + Mubadala + PIF/Humain + QIA/Qai) has
collectively committed >$200B of announced AI capital in 2024-2026** across infrastructure,
foundation-model, and frontier-lab equity stakes. Singapore (GIC + Temasek) is second-most active
but far more price-sensitive. SoftBank is concentrated on OpenAI/Stargate. CIC is geopolitically
off-limits for a US-based founder.

**National-data-multiplier framing** (paper §0.2): the 91-signal upper-bound means a sovereign's
licensed national corpus (Arabic, Mandarin-off-limits-here, Malay, Korean, etc.) can be used to
generate up to 91× training value per input pair without a licensing market — making it an
especially clean narrative for sovereigns building Arabic/regional foundation models (ALLaM 34B,
Fanar, JAIS, K2 Think V2).
