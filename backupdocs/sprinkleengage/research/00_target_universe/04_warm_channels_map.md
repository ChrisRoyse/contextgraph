# Warm-Channels Map — Target Universe (Wave 1, Agent 04)

**Scope.** This file maps the *warm-channel surface* for a Teleox.ai / TCT / DDA engagement: the specific named humans who could introduce Chris Royse into any of the Tier A / B / C target orgs defined in the mission brief. **The asset being carried through these channels is the primitive (paper §0.2 counting identity + embedder-pool extensibility — see `research/05_executive_synthesis/THE_PRIMITIVE.md`), not the case-study products.** Warm-channel accessibility is scored against probability-of-responding-to-a-primitive-first-pitch, not against willingness-to-buy-a-product. Every affiliation is verified against a dated public source.
**Methodology.** Web-verified April 2026. Sources are Tier 1 (institutional page), Tier 2 (major tech press), or Tier 3 (analyst / podcast). Unverified facts are flagged explicitly.
**Definition of "warm channel".** A person is counted here if AT LEAST ONE of the following holds:
1. Technically proximate to the paper's thesis (model collapse, data wall, compression, multi-embedder retrieval, DDA-adjacent).
2. Public, searchable contact surface (arXiv, OpenReview, X, institutional email, podcast invites).
3. Known to respond to cold technical outreach backed by a paper + reproducible artefact.

All 45+ humans below meet at least one criterion. Accessibility column encodes expected response-to-cold-paper probability on a 1–5 scale: 1 = essentially unreachable (e.g., Hassabis direct), 5 = public, cold-email-friendly, posts critique of incoming papers.
