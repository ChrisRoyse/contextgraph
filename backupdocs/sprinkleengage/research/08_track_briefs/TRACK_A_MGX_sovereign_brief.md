An April 2026 paper (arXiv-ready) formalizes a primitive that reshapes the data layer itself. **Derived Data Abundance (DDA)** (§0.2): project a corpus of `n` inputs through `N` frozen approximately-independent embedders; get **up to `n·(N + C(N,2))` structured supervisory signals — constructive upper bound, tightness pending EXP-2 (§9)**. At N=13 (the public embedder pool Chris has been working with today), up to **91 structured clean labeled supervisory signals per input**. **Every new embedder added to the pool multiplies correlations against every existing embedder** — and an embedder can be built to represent the meaning of anything: sovereign-specific dialects, regional prosody, national-legal-taxonomy, domain sensors, anything MGX or its portfolio can define. **Public embedder inventory is the constraint. The primitive is unbounded.**

The three case studies in the paper (Shakespeare LoRA public, Santa talking-head MEASURED operational, voice clone within-WavLM-family MEASURED) are exploratory proofs the primitive works across text, video, and audio identity. **They are not products being pitched.** They are evidence that the primitive produces deployable, scope-preserving results today — across independent modalities.

**For a sovereign**: every embedder you commission (national-dialect prosody, legal-clause semantics, cultural-entity graphs, domain-sensor fingerprints) extends the pool, multiplies correlations across the whole corpus, and compounds the national-data advantage. Every projection is deterministic, air-gap-compatible, per-output geometrically verifiable via G_τ (§5.4), and structurally outside the Shumailov-form recursion regime (§3.3, scope not refutation). **Your corpus never leaves your environment. Your embedder choices determine your ceiling.**

## 1. The Problem MGX Already Diagnosed

MGX's public "sovereignty of training signal" posture assumes three facts the frontier market is now pricing:

1. **The data wall is arithmetic.** Villalobos et al. (2024) estimate human-generated text exhausted 2026–2032 at current scaling — consistent with Kaplan et al. 2020.
2. **Licensing cost per new-signal is rising super-linearly.** News Corp ~$250M / 5yr, Reddit ~$60M/yr, Anthropic $1.5B settlement — incremental-signal economics have broken from any traditional content-licensing comparable.
3. **Recursion degrades frontier models.** Shumailov et al., Nature 2024: synthetic data generated by prior models induces distributional collapse, closing the synthetic-data escape hatch for any lab that cannot separate generator from training signal.

Consequence: whichever sovereign holds the most signal-dense licensed corpus **and can multiply it without recursion-collapse** controls the next training cycle. Abu Dhabi is positioned. The missing primitive is the multiplier.

## 2. The Proposal — DDA Counting Identity Applied to Sovereign Corpora

The paper's §0.2 identity is deliberately simple:

> Project a corpus of `n` inputs through a panel of `N` frozen, pre-trained, approximately-independent embedders. Count the directly-supervisable objects:
> - `n · N` per-embedder centroids (one per input per embedder),
> - `n · C(N,2)` per-embedder-pair agreement predicates,
>
> giving **up to `n · (N + C(N,2))` structured supervisory signals**.

At **N=13**, up to **91 signals per input** (constructive upper bound, tightness pending EXP-2, §9). At N=5 → 15; at N=20 → 210.

Three properties for a sovereign:

1. **Generator-free.** Embedders are frozen pre-trained projection functions; no model generates training text. The construction is **structurally outside the Shumailov-form recursion regime (scope, not refutation)** (§3.3).
2. **Per-output geometrically verifiable.** Every output scores against the centroid panel via G_τ (§5.4) — a per-output trust signal scalar-reward cannot furnish. **Different property class, not a benchmark contest** against RLHF/DPO/Constitutional AI (§2.5, §5.4).
3. **Corpus-preserving.** Only projections leave your infrastructure (or nothing leaves, on-prem). The native deployment shape for sovereign, air-gapped, or regulatory-sensitive data estates.

  environment. **ClipCannon** (67,585 LOC Python · 58 MCP tools · 23-stage DAG · 1,131 passing

  tables — no external dependency.

  Constellation** (SigLIP-SO400M 1,152 + Nomic 768 + Wav2Vec2 1,024 + WavLM 512 + prosody 12

  SHA-256 chain hash `SHA256("{parent_hash}|{input_sha256}|{output_sha256}|{operation}|
  {model}|{version}|{params}")`. `verify_chain` walks the chain and identifies any broken
  record. Detects accidental corruption and unauthorised partial edits — **an auditable

- **On-prem.** Runs on commodity GPU racks; no hyperscaler cloud dependency architecturally.
