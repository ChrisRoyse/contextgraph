# Discovery: The Named-Researcher Proof Anchor Dominates Recent Frontier Acqui-Hires

## Finding
Across the three canonical post-2024 frontier AI acqui-hires — Character.AI (Google $2.7B), Inflection (MSFT $650M), Adept (Amazon, ~$1B+ implied) — the deal dollar value was indexed to **one-to-three named researchers' publication track records**, not to product revenue, MAU, or ARR.

- **Character.AI → Shazeer**: "Attention is All You Need" (2017) first-author.
- **Inflection → Suleyman + Simonyan**: DeepMind co-founders.
- **Adept → Luan + Odena**: OpenAI GPT engineering leads; action-transformer research papers.

In **none** of these deals did the acquiring hyperscaler's corp-dev prioritise product metrics. In each case, the **named researcher** was the commercial anchor.

## Implication for Teleox
- Chris must position **himself as the named researcher**, not Teleox-as-product.
- The paper (*Teleological Constellation Training…*, April 2026) + Case 1 LoRA (huggingface.co/cabdru/shakespeare-lora-gemma4) + Case 3 0.961 MEASURED WavLM SECS anchor serve the same role as Shazeer's Transformer paper: they are the research credentials that make the acqui-hire structurally tractable.
- **Counter-implication**: for hyperscaler corp-dev, the first question is "who is this researcher, and what do they publish?" — the paper-first outreach is the correct primary channel, not the product demo.

## Second-order pattern: Advisor invisibility on acqui-hires
Traditional M&A (Nuance, Mandiant, Figma) lists named banks in public announcements. **Acqui-hires (Character, Inflection, Adept) list NO banks** in public disclosure.

- This is structural, not accidental: acqui-hires avoid proxy/S-4 filings (no public vote) → no requirement to disclose advisors.
- **Implication**: for a license-and-acqui-hire outcome, the "get-in" is NOT through a bank. It is through a **board member, sitting executive, or direct founder-to-corp-dev-head contact**.
- This changes the broker strategy: Tier-D (banks) is the backup plan for a traditional-M&A structure; Tier-A (direct exec access) is the primary for an acqui-hire structure.

## Third-order pattern: Regulator attention is now structural
Every post-2024 license-and-acqui-hire has drawn regulator inquiry:
- **Google–Character**: DOJ investigation (late 2024). [[1]](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/sy06wllflg)
- **Amazon–Adept**: FTC 6(b) information request (July 2024). [[2]](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/07/16/amazons-deal-with-ai-startup-adept-faces-ftc-scrutiny.html)
- **Microsoft–Inflection**: FTC + UK CMA review (2024).

The Figma–Adobe $1B breakup fee (2023) established the record reverse-termination fee for EU/UK antitrust blockage. Any Teleox $3B+ structure must pre-negotiate the regulatory posture.
