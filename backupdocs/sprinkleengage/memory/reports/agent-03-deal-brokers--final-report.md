## Key findings

### Finding 1 — Named-researcher proof anchor
Across Character/Inflection/Adept, deal value indexed to **named researchers' publication track records** (Shazeer/Suleyman/Luan), NOT to product metrics. Direct implication: Chris's paper + LoRA + Case 3 MEASURED anchor is the correct primary credential, not Teleox-as-product.

### Finding 2 — Advisor invisibility on acqui-hires
Traditional M&A (Nuance, Mandiant, Figma) lists named banks. **Acqui-hires (Character, Inflection, Adept) list NO banks.** Structural reason: no proxy/S-4 → no disclosure duty. Implication: for an L&A outcome, the "in" is NOT through a bank.

### Finding 3 — Centerview is Meta's chosen bank
Bloomberg confirmed Centerview as **sole Wall Street bank** on the Meta-Scale $14.3B deal (June 2025). If Meta is the target, Centerview is the named gatekeeper.

### Finding 4 — Goldman is ambidextrous
Goldman TMT ran **Microsoft-buy** (Nuance) and **Mandiant-sell** (Google). Tier-1 bank with cross-hyperscaler credibility. Dec-2025 TMT restructure (Coupal/Tofsky Infra-Tech; Watkins/Uppalapati Internet&Media) explicitly targets AI-infra deals.

### Finding 5 — Qatalyst is the only Tier-1 banker cold-accessible
Frank Quattrone is publicly visible (20VC Jan 2026, speaking circuit) and his framing ("650 deals, $1T aggregate, founder-direct") is an invitation. This is the single largest actionable tactic.

### Finding 6 — Regulator scrutiny is now structural
Every post-2024 L&A (Character, Inflection, Adept) drew DOJ/FTC inquiry. Figma–Adobe $1B breakup fee is the record. Any Teleox $3B+ must pre-negotiate regulatory posture.
