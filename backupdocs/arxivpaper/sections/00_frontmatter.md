abstract: |
  Frontier pre-training has approached the ceiling of high-quality public text, recursive self-generation induces irreversible drift under the cited collapse theorems, and a licensing race prices enterprise corpora in hundreds of millions per partnership; the standing responses --- acquire or synthesise --- remain inside the recursion. We ask whether a fixed corpus projected through $N$ frozen, approximately independently trained embedders can sustain training without closing a generator-in-loop, and name three constructs. \textbf{Derived Data Abundance (DDA)} is a counting identity under which $n$ inputs admit up to $n \cdot (N + \binom{N}{2})$ structured supervisory signals --- a constructive upper bound whose tightness is pending a pairwise mutual-information audit. \textbf{Meaning compression} is proposed as a fourth taxonomic entry alongside bit-, weight-, and activation-compression, semantically distinct in unit (signals per raw-data unit) rather than superior to them. \textbf{Teleological Constellation Training (TCT)} is a three-phase protocol whose runtime guard $G_\tau$ is a deterministic geometric test providing per-output verifiability relative to the chosen centroid panel. Two production systems instantiate the substrate: Context Graph ($\approx$370{,}000-line Rust workspace; 13 embedders; 59 RocksDB column families; 75 MCP tools; up to 91 signals per input) and ClipCannon ($\approx$67{,}585-line Python package; 23-stage DAG; 7 modalities $\times$ 4{,}044 dimensions; 58 MCP tools; up to 28 signals per input). Three case studies read the framework back. Case 3 is measured at \textbf{0.961 mean WavLM SECS (max 0.975) on 10 held-out English sentences for one speaker under an encoder-matched protocol}, with DNSMOS P.808 parity (3.93 / 3.93) and UTMOS parity (3.012 / 2.997) on a 14-sentence set. Case 1 (Shakespeare stylistic LoRA on Gemma-4-E4B-it at rank 256) is measured end-to-end at \textbf{5/5 Full State Verification PASS} (love-poem, quantum-entanglement-in-verse, prompt-injection refusal in character, Go binary-search with Shakespeare comments, and a novel zero-shot cross-lingual period-style transfer to Golden Age Spanish observed on a Spanish prompt); the trained LoRA is published at \texttt{huggingface.co/cabdru/shakespeare-lora-gemma4}. Case 2 (identity-locked talking-head clone) remains projected, with re-verification flagged for a subsequent version. DDA pipelines are structurally outside the Shumailov-form recursion regime (scope, not refutation). Open problems: cross-encoder SECS triangulation (ECAPA-TDNN), a pairwise embedder mutual-information audit, and an $n_m$ sample-complexity sweep.
