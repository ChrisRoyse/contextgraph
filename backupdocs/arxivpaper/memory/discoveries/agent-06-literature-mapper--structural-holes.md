# Structural Holes — Combined Gap Map

This file consolidates the 18 per-cluster structural holes identified in
`--topic-topology.md` into **five combined holes** that the arXiv submission explicitly
positions itself to fill. These five holes feed §1 Introduction (hole → three-paths
framing) and §2 Related Work (hole → novelty-delta row per cluster, G3 table).

Per P07 / G3, each hole is expressed as a **narrow structural delta**, not an AGI claim
(FM-03) and not a superlative ("best ever"). Each hole is traced to at least one Tier-A
construct (glossary #1–#4) and at least one essential question (Q01–Q20).

---

## HOLE-1. The signals-per-input ratio is unformalised.

**Source clusters.** A-HOLE-1, A-HOLE-3, C-HOLE-1, C-HOLE-2, D-HOLE-2.

**Statement.** Prior work on the data wall (Cluster A) frames the bottleneck as a raw
input count (~300T tokens exhausted 2026–2032). Prior work on compression-as-intelligence
(Cluster C) reads compression from trained models, not from training-data preparation.
Prior work on multi-modal embeddings (Cluster D) treats embedders as retrieval tools,
not as labellers. **No cited work formalises a labelled-signal-per-unit-of-raw-data
ratio** as the operative quantity when the raw-input supply is fixed.

**What our paper fills.** §3.1 introduces this ratio as the definition of **meaning
compression** (glossary #2 / Q05) — a fourth compression category orthogonal to bit-,
weight-, and activation-compression. §3.4 makes it quantitative via the DDA count
$n(N + \binom{N}{2})$.

**Tier-A constructs covered.** DDA (#1), meaning compression (#2).

**Essential questions answered.** Q04 (DDA formal), Q05 (four-category taxonomy).

**Novelty-delta phrasing (for agent-36 §2 G3 table, row 1).** "The cited compression
taxonomy has three categories (bit, weight, activation) measured in the same unit
column per category. None has units *labelled signals per unit of raw data*. Meaning
compression (§3.1) is dimensionally orthogonal to all three."

## HOLE-2. Decomposition through N frozen embedders is not on the data-wall menu.

**Source clusters.** A-HOLE-2, B-HOLE-1, B-HOLE-2, C-HOLE-3, D-HOLE-1.

**Statement.** The data-wall literature (Cluster A) offers two paths: **path 1 — acquire
more inputs** (license more corpora; Chinchilla-rebalance; robots.txt-restricted Internet;
Cluster A + Cluster E in `_evidence_04`) and **path 2 — synthesize new inputs from a
generator** (risks model collapse; Cluster B). The model-collapse literature (Cluster B)
treats the unit of analysis as "was this sample generated by a prior model" and does not
distinguish between generating new inputs and computing new labels from frozen
independently-trained embedders on unchanged real inputs. Retrieval literature (Cluster D)
uses embedders as rankers, not as labellers. **No cited work names a third path that is
neither acquisition nor synthesis** — i.e., decomposition of existing inputs through
$N$ frozen embedders to extract $N + \binom{N}{2}$ supervisory signals without a
generator loop.

**What our paper fills.** §1 Introduction names the three paths and places DDA on the
third. §3.3 makes the structural argument explicit: DDA has no generative feedback loop;
the Shumailov (2024) collapse theorem is silent on DDA. First-use rule V15 enforces the
"not synthetic data" clause within two sentences of every DDA mention.

**Tier-A constructs covered.** DDA (#1), third path around the data wall (glossary #27).

**Essential questions answered.** Q01 (data-wall framing), Q02 (DDA vs. model collapse),
Q03 (existing mitigations → gap), Q04 (DDA count).

**Novelty-delta phrasing (for agent-36 §2 G3 table, rows 2–3).** "Model-collapse
mitigations (Gerstgrasser et al., 2024; Feng et al., 2024) operate *within* a generator-
feedback loop (accumulate vs. verify). DDA sits *outside* that loop: it decomposes a
fixed corpus through N frozen independently-trained embedders. The collapse theorem is
silent on it."

## HOLE-3. Per-output runtime geometric guard against a frozen centroid is absent.

**Source clusters.** D-HOLE-3, E-HOLE-2, F-HOLE-2.

**Statement.** Alignment literature (implicit in §2.6 of our Related Work) optimises a
**learned scalar reward** against preference pairs (RLHF / DPO / Constitutional AI) with
no per-output inference-time check. Retrieval literature (Cluster D) uses embedders as
similarity scorers but not as runtime acceptance gates. Zero-shot TTS literature
(Cluster E) reports SIM-o / WavLM-SECS *after* generation on the whole held-out set; no
cited system (VALL-E 2, NS3, MaskGCT, F5-TTS, Voicebox, NS2, Mega-TTS 2, XTTS) resamples
against a per-utterance cosine threshold. Identity-locked video generation (Cluster F)
evaluates lip-sync and visual quality *after* generation; no cited system (EchoMimicV3,
Hallo3, LatentSync, HunyuanVideo-Avatar, EMO/EMO2, CogVideoX, Wan) applies a runtime
per-modality cosine gate. **No cited work in any of these four clusters proposes a
runtime validator that re-embeds every generated output through the same frozen
embedders used to build the alignment target, computes per-modality cosine similarities
to a frozen centroid, and accepts or rejects per output.**

**What our paper fills.** §5.3 introduces the **constellation guard** (glossary #12) as
the inference-time half of TCT (glossary #3). Case 3 (§6.3) instantiates it concretely
(WavLM-scored best-of-12 at T=0.3 against the 50-clip speaker centroid). Case 2 (§6.2)
projects the same guard across five modalities (visual, audio-speaker, audio-voice,
emotion, FLAME/expression) per the ClipCannon seven-modality DAG (glossary #23).

**Tier-A constructs covered.** TCT (#3), identity-locked generation (#4), constellation
guard (glossary #12).

**Essential questions answered.** Q06 (TCT vs. RLHF/DPO/CAI), Q07 (three-phase TCT
protocol), Q09 (TCT vs. CLIP/SigLIP/WavLM/VALL-E 2), Q15 (sample-complexity collapse
under guard).

**Novelty-delta phrasing (for agent-36 §2 G3 table, row 4).** "Scalar-reward alignment
(RLHF, DPO, CAI) and zero-shot TTS / identity-locked video evaluate similarity after
generation. TCT adds a per-output constellation guard: re-embed through the same frozen
embedders, compute per-modality cosines, accept iff all ≥ τ_m. The guard is both the
enforcer *and* the detector — a property the cited comparators do not share."

## HOLE-4. Centroid aggregation from a multi-clip reference set is not the convention.

**Source clusters.** E-HOLE-1, F-HOLE-1.

**Statement.** Zero-shot TTS (Cluster E) is conventionally a *single-prompt* task:
VALL-E 2 (Chen et al., 2024), NS3, MaskGCT, F5-TTS, XTTS, Mega-TTS 2 all condition on
one reference utterance or an arbitrary-length single prompt. Identity-locked video
generation (Cluster F) is conventionally a *reference-image + audio* task: EMO / EMO2,
Hallo3, LatentSync, HunyuanVideo-Avatar, EchoMimicV3 condition on one or a few
reference images, not on a multi-modal centroid set. **No cited system aggregates a
multi-clip reference set into an L2-normalised centroid per modality and holds that
centroid frozen through training and inference** — even though the frozen-centroid
aggregation is a trivially available operation on top of the cited embedders.

**What our paper fills.** §5.1 formalises the centroid-construction phase. Case 3 uses
a **50-clip reference set → WavLM L2-normalised centroid** (glossary #7 / #9). Case 2
projects the same aggregation across the ClipCannon seven modalities. The move is
structural, not heroic: the arithmetic is standard L2-normalised mean of L2-normalised
embeddings; what matters is the downstream use as a *frozen alignment target* rather
than a retrieval query.

**Tier-A constructs covered.** TCT (#3), centroid (glossary #7), constellation
(glossary #8), reference set (glossary #9), frozen (glossary #10).

**Essential questions answered.** Q07 (three-phase TCT protocol, phase-1), Q08
(threshold calibration), Q12 (Case 3 measured numbers — centroid is what the 0.961 SECS
is *relative to*), Q15 (sample-complexity collapse from "thousands of subject-specific
examples" to "one reference set per modality + frozen constellation").

**Novelty-delta phrasing (for agent-36 §2 G3 table, row 5).** "Zero-shot TTS and
identity-locked video condition on a single prompt / reference image. TCT aggregates a
multi-clip reference set into a frozen L2-normalised centroid per modality and uses the
constellation as the alignment target."

## HOLE-5. Flow-matching sign-convention and single-subject evaluation forensics are
undocumented in the cited prior work.

**Source clusters.** F-HOLE-3, E-HOLE-3 (adjacent: WavLM same-session band).

**Statement.** Flow-matching generative modelling (Lipman et al., 2023) and flow-matching
video (EchoMimicV3 / Wan; Cluster F) do not publish the specific sign convention
$\text{target} = \text{noise} - \text{latent}$ as a forensic hazard; flipping the sign
causes anti-denoising. No cited paper names this. Similarly, the WavLM same-session
band (0.95–0.99 within one speaker's own recordings) is empirically observable but has
no canonical citation (`_evidence_02` § 8). **No cited work in our six clusters provides
the two forensic artefacts that our paper discloses by reference**: (a) the flow-matching
sign-convention forensic (Santa project `docs/inspect/08_training_audit.md`), and (b)
the Shakespeare training-data echo-bug FSV log (15/15 passes post-clean). P10 / FM-08
require these disclosures.

**What our paper fills.** §6.1 Case 1 includes the echo-bug forensic paragraph. §6.2
Case 2 includes the flow-matching sign-bug forensic paragraph (glossary #30). §8.4
aggregates forensic lessons. These are not contributions to the generative-modelling
theory; they are **contributions to the replicator experience** — a reader attempting
to reproduce our results needs to know these specific hazards exist.

**Tier-A constructs covered.** Forensic report (glossary #29), flow-matching sign
convention (glossary #30), identity-locked generation (#4 — because the bugs gated
identity-lock quality).

**Essential questions answered.** Q13 (Case 1 and Case 2 projected metrics + the
pipeline history that produced them), Q17 (forensic failures that preceded the working
method), Q18 (replicator reproducibility notes).

**Novelty-delta phrasing (for agent-36 §2 G3 table, row 6).** "Flow-matching video
generation papers do not document the sign-convention hazard that caused anti-denoising
in our Case 2 pipeline. We disclose the forensic file by reference and state the
convention in glossary terms."
