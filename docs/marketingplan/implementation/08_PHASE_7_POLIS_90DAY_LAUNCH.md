# Phase 7 — Polis 90-Day Operational Launch (Weeks 29-40)

> The third launch. Fundamentally different from Leapable/Synapse: **consumer social, in-person, network-effect-driven**. Single-city seed (Cambridge/Somerville), campus ambassadors, TikTok-first content, BeReal-pattern density. Goal: `monthly_active_event_attendances ≥ 6,000` over last 28d. Operator-time-heavy + physical-presence-required. Cannot start until Leapable + Synapse are in stable steady-state because operator bandwidth is the hard constraint.

**Source spec docs:** `11_PRODUCT_PLAYBOOKS.md §3`, `13_CONSUMER_SOCIAL_LAUNCH.md`, `17_LAUNCH_PLAYBOOK_90DAY.md §5`, `09_VIRAL_LOOPS_AND_REFERRALS.md §3.3`, `18_CASE_STUDIES.md §4 §5`.

## §1. Phase summary

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | 90 days (weeks 29-40) |
| **Engineering concurrency** | None — Phase 4 complete; Leapable + Synapse in steady-state |
| **Prerequisites** | Leapable goal achieved; Synapse goal achieved; operator commits to seed-city physical presence; Polis ethical commitments signed per `14 §6` |

## §2. The 3-phase consumer-social pattern

Polis pattern is fundamentally different from dev tools (per `13`):

| Phase | Days | Pattern | End-of-phase target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Single-city seed** | 0-30 | Cambridge/Somerville only. 3-5 events/wk capped at 12 attendees. | 200 unique attendees, friend-cluster density ≥3 |
| **B — Densification** | 30-60 | Same city. Ambassador program. Two-prize giveaway. | 200 attendees, NPS ≥50 |
| **C — Geographic expansion + mega-launch** | 60-90 | Open 3 cities (Boston, NYC, Brooklyn). TikTok mega-launch. | 1,500-2,000 attendees across 4 cities; 6,000+ monthly_active_event_attendances |

## §3. Phase A — Single-city seed (Days 0-30)

### 3.1 City selection

Cambridge/Somerville is the leading candidate per `17 §5.1`:
- MIT + Harvard density
- Intellectual culture
- Walkable scale
- Operator local presence

Alternatives if operator not Boston-based: Austin (campus + intellectual culture), Berkeley (Bay Area indie), NYC LES/Williamsburg cluster.

**Operator decision required before Day 1.**

### 3.2 Headline activities

- Operator + 2 hand-picked ambassadors recruited locally
- 3-5 trial events / week, capped at 12 attendees
- Event formats: salon, debate, third-place revival, knowledge-growth session, art-walk
- Every attendee invited to bring ≥1 friend to next event
- Light branded swag (postcard with "Sarah brought you" attribution)
- TikTok organic: 3-5 short clips/day from Polis-persona account
- IG organic: 1 post/day cross-posted from TikTok
- Lead magnet: "Third Place Revival Guide" PDF (free, intellectually substantive)

### 3.3 Week-by-week

**Week 29 (Days 1-7) — Trial events + ambassador onboarding.**
- Operator hosts 1 trial event (6-10 people, salon format)
- 2 ambassadors recruited (intellectual curiosity + local friend density)
- TikTok account created; first 5 organic clips
- Lead magnet PDF live on operator's site + Polis-persona Substack

**Week 30 (Days 8-14) — Cadence ramp.**
- 2 events/week (Tue + Sat)
- Operator hosts; ambassadors invite their friends
- First "Sarah brought you" postcard tested
- Brand voice doc for Polis-persona finalized

**Week 31 (Days 15-21) — Cadence + friend density.**
- 3 events/week (Tue, Thu, Sat)
- Friend-of-friend density compounds; aim for 2 friend clusters with overlap
- TikTok scaling: ~3 clips/day
- First Substack creator outreach drafted (operator-sent week 32)

**Week 32 (Days 22-30) — 5-event week + Phase A predicate review.**
- 5 events/week (different formats)
- 50 unique attendees, 4 events with ≥2 friend-clusters, 65% show rate target
- Two-prize giveaway pattern introduced on week-32 events (Hormozi/Skool from `09 §5.2`)
- Operator wraps Phase A; reviews ambassador performance; sample-audits attendee experience

### 3.4 Phase A end-state

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| `monthly_active_event_attendances` (Cambridge) | ≥ 200 |
| Events held | ≥ 17 (over 30 days) |
| Friend cluster density avg | ≥ 3 |
| Show rate (attended ÷ RSVP'd) | ≥ 65% |
| TikTok views (total) | ≥ 25k |
| TikTok followers | ≥ 200 |
| Lead magnet downloads | ≥ 100 |
| Ambassadors active | 2-3 |

## §4. Phase B — Densification (Days 30-60)

### 4.1 Headline activities

- Same single city (Cambridge); friend density compounds
- 5 events/week sustained, still capped at 12 attendees
- Two-prize giveaway pattern at every event
- First campus ambassadors paid out (BeReal pattern $30/$50 per `13 §5.1`)
- Hormozi-volume TikTok: 5 vertical clips/day
- First Substack intellectual creator outreach (operator-sent; Pre-Call Briefer prepares)
- First podcast pitch (intellectual/culture, not tech)

### 4.2 Week-by-week

**Week 33 (Days 31-37) — Ambassador economics live.**
- $30/successful referral, $50/install+attended-event per `13 §5.1`
- Bonus tier at 25+ activations: $500 lump sum
- Cap of 5 ambassadors per institution in seed phase
- Ambassador-only Discord activated

**Week 34 (Days 38-44) — TikTok scaling.**
- 5 vertical clips/day from Polis-persona account
- David Aliages watchlist (per `13 §6.3`): if non-affiliated creator posts about Polis, operator personally reaches out
- IG cross-posts continue
- First "I went to my first Polis event" testimonial from a real attendee (consented)

**Week 35 (Days 45-51) — Substack outreach + first podcast pitch.**
- Mapper-identified bridges to Tier-2 intellectual creators
- 5 Substack creators contacted: Erik Hoel, Astral Codex Ten, Robin Hanson, Tyler Cowen-adjacent econ-blog cluster
- Pre-Call Briefer prepares each outreach
- First podcast pitch sent (intellectual/culture, not tech)

**Week 36 (Days 52-60) — Phase B review.**
- 200 unique attendees in Cambridge sustained
- Friend cluster density ≥3 avg
- NPS ≥50 (anonymous post-event survey)
- 1+ Substack creator agreed to interview swap

### 4.3 Phase B end-state

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| `monthly_active_event_attendances` (Cambridge) | ≥ 200 over last 28d |
| Ambassadors active | ≥ 3 |
| Ambassador payouts to date | ≥ $1,500 |

## §5. Phase C — Geographic expansion + mega-launch (Days 60-90)

### 5.1 Headline activities

- Day 60: open registration in 3 expansion cities (Boston, NYC, Brooklyn)
- 3 ambassadors per expansion city (recruited weeks 33-36)
- Operator-attended launch event in each new city (3 events in 7 days)
- TikTok mega-launch: 5+ video drops + amplifying any organic creator who covered Polis
- Press: tier-2 culture press (not tech press)
- Substack creator partnerships activated for revenue share

### 5.2 Week-by-week

**Week 37 (Days 61-67) — Expansion city prep.**
- 3 ambassadors per new city onboarded
- Each ambassador hosts 1 trial event week 37
- Cambridge cadence continues at 5/week

**Week 38 (Days 68-74) — Operator-attended launches.**
- Operator physically attends 1 launch event in each of Boston, NYC, Brooklyn (3 events over 7 days)
- Each launch event ≥20 attendees
- Local press attendance pitched

**Week 39 (Days 75-81) — Mega-launch week.**
- 5+ TikTok video drops in 5 days
- Influence amplification: organic-creator outreach + amplification
- Lead-magnet promoted via Tier-2 Substack creators
- Coordinated PH submission (consumer-app angle) — optional, less critical than Leapable/Synapse PH launches

**Week 40 (Days 82-90) — Post-launch + Phase C predicate review.**
- 4-city cadence sustained
- Predicate target: 1,500-2,000 unique attendees across 4 cities; 6,000+ monthly_active_event_attendances

### 5.3 Phase C end-state

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| `monthly_active_event_attendances` | ≥ 6,000 over last 28d |
| Active cities | 4 (Cambridge, Boston, NYC, Brooklyn) |
| Ambassadors active | ≥ 11 (2-3 Cambridge + 3 per expansion city) |
| TikTok followers | ≥ 10,000 |
| Substack creator partnerships | ≥ 3 active |
| Press mentions (tier-2 culture press) | ≥ 3 |

| 28d attendee return rate | ≥ 50% |

## §10. Polis-specific hard rules (per `13 §10`)

These are doctrine, no override:

- **No fake attendees** in events (instant-kill brand event)
- **No paid first-attendee** (attendees come because they want to)
- **No undisclosed paid event hosts** (compensation disclosed in post-event comms)
- **No demographic targeting beyond legal/ethical norms** (cities target geographically; inclusive)
- **Photo / quote consent at every event** (default opt-out, explicit opt-in for promotion)
- **No event recording without explicit attendee consent**
- **Anti-engagement-farming enforcement on operator's content** (operator's Polis-persona is strict; no engagement-bait)

## §12. Risks specific to Phase 7

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operator cannot sustain seed-city physical presence | Medium | Critical | If operator commit fails, pause Polis launch (hard doctrine per `13 §12`) |
| Cambridge seed doesn't reach friend-density threshold by Phase A end | Medium | High | Extend Phase A; do not advance to Phase B until density holds |
| Ambassador pay-out economics drift (ambassadors gaming) | Medium | Medium | Per-ambassador limits; manual review of high-volume ambassadors |
| TikTok-viral attempt produces zero hits | Medium | Low | Continue trying; volume of attempts (5/day) provides shots-on-goal |
| Substack creator partnerships stall | Medium | Medium | Operator drives directly; bridge-cultivation cadence continues |
| Bad attendee incident (harassment, complaint) | Low | Critical | Anti-harassment policy live before Phase A; named contact; clear consequences |
| Photo-consent failures | Low | Medium | Default opt-out enforced; agents drafting attendee posts auto-check consent flag |
| Polis predicate inflated by ambassador-only attendance (Goodhart) | Medium | High | Auditor checks: return-rate, NPS, friend-graph density delta — must move with attendance |
| Expansion-city ambassadors underperform Cambridge | High | Medium | Operator-attended launches set the cultural template; iterate ambassador playbook |
| Leapable / Synapse regress during operator-presence-heavy Polis Phase A | Medium | High | Steady-state cycles handle without operator intervention; cross-goal scheduler protects |
| Local press declines or covers negatively | Medium | Low | No press dependency for predicate; nice-to-have not need-to-have |
