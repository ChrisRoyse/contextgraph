## 2. His core theory — exact claims and citations

### 2.1 Mass–energy–information equivalence principle (2019)
- **Vopson, M. M. (2019). The mass-energy-information equivalence principle. *AIP Advances*, 9(9), 095206.** https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0019421
- Claim: a bit of information has finite mass; at 300 K, `m_bit ≈ 3.19 × 10⁻³⁸ kg`. Information is **"the fifth state of matter."** A full 1 TB device is predicted to be ~`2.5 × 10⁻²⁵ kg` heavier than erased.
- **Experimental test (2022):** electron–positron annihilation should emit, beyond the two 511 keV gamma photons, **two extra low-energy (~50 μm IR) photons** from erasure of the particles' information content. *This experiment is IPI's stated prime objective; they are seeking funding/partners to run it.* — Vopson, *Experimental protocol for testing the mass–energy–information equivalence principle*, AIP Advances (2022), https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0087175

### 2.2 Second law of infodynamics (2022) — THE load-bearing one for you
- **Vopson, M. M., & Lepadatu, S. (2022). Second law of information dynamics. *AIP Advances*, 12(7), 075310.** https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0100358
- Claim: in a closed system containing information states, **information entropy remains constant or *decreases* over time, reaching a minimum at equilibrium** — the *opposite* of the thermodynamic second law. Demonstrated on digital data storage and an **RNA genome** (SARS-CoV-2 mutations minimise information entropy → mutations are not purely random).
- **This is the paper your `L_entropy` loss (#511) and `CP_Φ` compression-progress metric formalise. Cite it as the theoretical anchor of the joint work.**

### 2.3 Simulated-universe paper (2023) — the symmetry/entropy link you referenced
- **Vopson, M. M. (2023). The second law of infodynamics and its implications for the simulated universe hypothesis. *AIP Advances*, 13(10), 105308.** https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0173278
- Extends the law to **digital info, genetic info, atomic physics (Hund's rule / electron orbital filling), mathematical symmetry, and cosmology.**
- **Key result for you:** *"high symmetry is a preferred state in nature because high symmetry corresponds to low information entropy content."* This is the precise statement your "panel collapses toward higher-symmetry, lower-entropy slot configurations" hypothesis maps onto. (Note: in your first email you called it "your 2026 Entropy paper on symmetry of Euclidean polygons" — the canonical symmetry result is actually in this **2023 AIP Advances** paper. Cite it correctly.)
