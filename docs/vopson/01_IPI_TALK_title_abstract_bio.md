>
> Where this meets information physics: a well-designed panel is built to maximise *differentiated information per instrument* — each must add new bits, and a redundant instrument is noise and is retired (Shannon information made an engineering contract, measured in bits against a real outcome). > I will show this running across three production systems (panels of 7, 13, and 21 frozen instruments, spanning video, code, and civic matching) and present measured results: a voice reproduced at 0.961 mean speaker-embedding similarity (encoder-matched — I will state the cross-encoder caveat plainly) and a style model that holds character under adversarial prompting (5/5 verification, public weights).
