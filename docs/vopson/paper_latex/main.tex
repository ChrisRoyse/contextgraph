\noindent\textbf{Abstract.}
This paper advances one claim: intelligence is the measurement and composition of
associations. A dictionary cannot define a word with a word---each entry sends you
to others, a merry-go-round of symbols that never bottoms out (Harnad's symbol
grounding problem)---yet it works, because meaning is carried by the
\emph{associations} between words, not the words: model a dictionary as a graph and
$\approx 99\%$ is definable from a grounding kernel of $\approx 1\%$. So meaning is
grounded association. Human intelligence runs this calculus on a biological
substrate; \emph{artificial general intelligence is the same calculus written
down}, and building it is what this paper does. The instrument is a \emph{frozen
embedder}: trained on a corpus and frozen, it stops being a vectoriser and becomes
a measurement device that reports where an input sits in that corpus's web of
associations. The lens is manufacturable---commission one for any axis a corpus
exists for---so a panel of them is a designable basis for meaning, general because
the instruments cover any domain and the cross-terms bridge across domains.

Three constructs follow, each formalised and running in production. \emph{Derived
Data Abundance}: $n$ real inputs through $N$ frozen, approximately-independent
instruments yield up to $n(N+\binom{N}{2}+1)$ structured signals, the
$\binom{N}{2}$ cross-terms being associations \emph{between} associations.
\emph{Meaning compression} is the per-input yield, complementary to the
compression-as-intelligence tradition. \emph{Teleological Constellation Training}
reuses the same panel as a frozen alignment target with a per-output cosine guard
$G_\tau$. Together they give an operational account of definition: a term is the
constellation the other instruments form at one instrument's index---G\"ardenfors's
object category, instantiated at scale with an information-theoretic
differentiation contract (every instrument must add ${\ge}0.05$ bits about a real
outcome; no pair may correlate above $0.6$).

Three systems instantiate the panel at growing size: ClipCannon (video, $N=7$),
Context Graph (text and code, $N=13$, up to 92 signals/input), and socialmedia2.com
(civic matching, $N=21$). Measured anchors: a voice reproduced at $0.961$ mean
WavLM speaker-similarity (encoder-matched; DNSMOS 3.93/3.93) and a published style
model that holds character under prompt injection with an emergent zero-shot
transfer to Golden-Age Spanish. The bound is stated plainly: the calculus computes
only associations present in the chosen corpora, the $\binom{N}{2}$ abundance is an
upper bound under approximate independence capped by the data-processing
inequality, and the gain is reduced sample complexity realised up to the panel's
effective rank.

\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Intelligence is the calculus of association}
\label{sec:thesis}
Open a dictionary and try to find where meaning is stored. You cannot define a
word with a word: every definition is built of other words, whose definitions are
built of others, and the trip is a merry-go-round of symbols that never comes to
rest on what anything means. \citet{harnad1990symbol} named this the
\emph{symbol grounding problem} and made it vivid with the Chinese/Chinese
dictionary-go-round: with only a monolingual dictionary in a language you do not
speak, you pass endlessly from one meaningless string to another. The words, in
themselves, are empty. And yet a dictionary works. What carries the meaning is not
the words but the \emph{associations} between them---which word is invoked to
define which. \citet{vincentlamarre2016latent} made this quantitative: model a
dictionary as a directed graph of definitional links and roughly $99\%$ of it can
be defined, by association alone, from a grounding kernel of about $1\%$ (a
minimum feedback vertex set) that must be anchored outside language. The mental
lexicon has the same shape: a small-world, scale-free association graph that grows
by differentiation \citep{steyvers2005largescale}.

That is the thesis of this paper, stated at full strength. \emph{Intelligence is
the measurement and composition of associations}; everything else---search,
planning, generation---is downstream of it. Human intelligence is this calculus
run on a biological substrate; \emph{artificial general intelligence is the same
calculus written down}---a designable, grounded, mathematical form of it---and
building that form is what this paper does. The word \emph{general} is not a boast
but a consequence: because the instruments are commissionable for any axis of
association and the cross-terms bridge any pair of domains, the same machine spans
video, code, and civic matching without retraining a new model per task. A single
representation, like a single word, means nothing in isolation; meaning is the structured relation between
representations, anchored at a small set of grounded points. It follows that a
system which can systematically \emph{measure} associations (project an object
through many lenses), \emph{count} them (the combinatorial cross-terms),
\emph{differentiate} them (keep only those that carry new grounded information),
and \emph{compose} them (name regions and gate against them) is running the engine
of intelligence itself. The rest of this paper builds that engine, formalises it
as a \emph{calculus of association}, and measures it in three production systems.
The one load-bearing caveat---association is circular unless it is grounded, and
what grounds ours is contact with real outcomes---is stated in
\S\ref{sec:reframe-honest}. \S\ref{sec:agi} then shows that the field's leading
accounts of intelligence---compression, generalisation, world-modelling, and
breadth-times-depth---are four projections of this one mechanism, and
Table~\ref{tab:caps} maps every capability the field asks of AGI to a construct
within it.

\subsection{The data wall is a symptom of counting the wrong thing}
The same lens explains the crisis the field is most worried about. Frontier
pre-training has nearly reached the ceiling of high-quality public text.
\citet{villalobos2022data} put the indexed human-text stock at roughly $300$
trillion tokens and project exhaustion somewhere between 2026 and 2032, sooner
under over-training regimes. \citet{longpre2024consent} watched a quarter of the
high-quality C4 tokens become restricted in a single year. The most-discussed
response is to train on generator-synthesised data, and that road is bounded:
\citet{shumailov2024collapse} show in \emph{Nature} that recursive
self-generation drives an irreversible distributional drift they call model
collapse. The commercial consequence is a data-licensing race running to the
hundreds of millions per partnership. So the standing responses divide cleanly.
Path~1 buys more real data. Path~2 makes more synthetic data. Both count
Shannon-compressed tokens, and both stay inside the collapse-prone recursion the
moment they generate. The wall is what you hit when you count tokens instead of
the associations among them; this paper counts the associations.

\subsection{The reframe: a frozen embedder is a designable lens of association}
This paper takes a third path, and it starts by asking what an embedder actually
\emph{is}. Training an embedder on a corpus and then freezing its weights does
not store the corpus. It stores the geometry of how everything in that corpus
related to everything else. The frozen weights are the associational structure of
the training data. So when a new input $x$ is projected through a frozen $\phi$,
the output $\phi(x)$ is not an encoding of $x$ at all. It is a measurement of $x$
against that whole web of associations, returning $x$'s coordinates inside it.

Three moves stack on this observation, and they organise the paper.
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep0.15em
\item \emph{One frozen embedder is one designable lens of association.} The
corpus you train on is the lens you get. So the instrument is manufacturable. A
``legal-risk'' lens is a model frozen on legal text; an ``affective-resonance''
lens is one frozen on annotated affect. You are not stuck with off-the-shelf
semantics. You can commission an instrument for any axis of association you can
assemble a corpus for. The lens is designed by choosing what to remember.
\item \emph{$N$ lenses are $N$ independent association-spaces, and the
$\binom{N}{2}$ cross-terms are associations between associations.} The pairwise
term does not measure the object twice. It measures how the object's position in
lens A relates to its position in lens B. That is a structured-analogy operator,
and it can relate two things that share no surface features at all, because the
relation lives in latent, transitive association.
\item \emph{A centroid plus a threshold is a label; a centroid set plus its
cross-terms is a definition.} Name a region of an association-space (a
constellation centroid), gate membership with $G_\tau$, and continuous geometry
becomes a discrete, countable, auditable label---meaning compression in one line.
Add the cross-terms and the label hardens into a \emph{definition}: the position
in any one lens is only an index, and the meaning is the constellation of
positions the other lenses report at that index (\S\ref{sec:definition}).
\end{enumerate}
Section~\ref{sec:reframe} develops the reframe and its consequences in full; the
rest of the paper formalises and measures them.

\subsection{The three constructs and the contribution}
Derived Data Abundance (DDA) is the principle that a fixed corpus of $n$ inputs,
projected through a panel of $N$ approximately-independent frozen instruments,
yields $n\cdot(N+\binom{N}{2}+1)$ structured supervisory signals at the dataset
level for a code substrate, which works out to as many as 92 per input at $N=13$
once a chunk-foundationality scalar is included. Meaning compression is the ratio
view of that same fact---structured-signal-yield per input---which I situate as a
complementary measure within the compression-as-intelligence tradition rather
than a rival taxonomic category. Teleological Constellation Training
(TCT) is the three-phase method that builds a frozen multi-modal centroid set
$C_P$, trains a generator against it with a geometric-proximity loss, and
enforces a per-output cosine guard $G_\tau$ at inference. The same frozen panel
plays three roles in TCT (retriever, labeller, runtime guard) with no parameter
updates.

So the contribution is one architectural move, three consequences, and one
reframe. The move is to use a single frozen embedder panel as retriever,
labeller, and runtime guard at once, over identical parameter instances. The
consequences are a counting identity ($|D'| = n\cdot(N+\binom{N}{2}+1)$, up to 92
at $N=13$; the multi-view interaction counted explicitly, not a new quantity), a
constructive sample-complexity bound
($\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{TCT}}(P)=O(M\cdot k)$), and a per-output verifiability
property that scalar-reward alignment does not provide. The reframe is that all
three are best read as a calculus of association over manufacturable measurement
instruments. That reading is what makes the method general across video, code,
and civic matching, and what ties it to the information-physics tradition in
which information is a physical, countable substrate. Pushed one step further it
is a theory of \emph{definition} (\S\ref{sec:definition}): a term is the
constellation the other instruments form at a given lens's index, taken over its
reference set---an operational, relational realisation of G\"ardenfors's geometry
of concepts (\S\ref{sec:related}).

The claim is precise. A frozen embedder panel used as retriever, labeller, and
runtime guard admits a counting identity with a constructive upper bound, a
sample-complexity theorem with measured operating points, and a per-output
verifiability property. All of it is bounded by the associations present in the
chosen corpora, and all of it is auditable. The counting identity bounds
extractable \emph{structure}, not information: by the data-processing inequality
(\S\ref{sec:dpi}) the panel cannot exceed the corpus's information about any real
outcome, so DDA's operative benefit is reduced sample complexity, and the realised
yield tracks the panel's effective rank rather than the nominal $\binom{N}{2}$.

\section{The Reframe: Frozen Embedders as a Calculus of Association}
\label{sec:reframe}
This section is the conceptual heart of the paper. It states what the later
sections formalise.

\subsection{An embedder is a frozen crystallisation of its corpus's associations}
Let $\phi$ be trained on corpus $D_\phi$ and then frozen
($\partial\phi/\partial\theta = 0$). The learned geometry of $\phi$ encodes the
relational statistics of $D_\phi$: which things co-occur, entail, resemble,
precede, or contrast with which, at whatever level of abstraction the training
objective rewarded. Freezing converts a learner into an instrument, a fixed
function whose output coordinates report a new input's position relative to the
associational structure $\phi$ memorised. Because the instrument is deterministic
and frozen, every later measurement is reproducible and auditable. That is the
precondition for treating $\phi(x)$ as a supervisory signal rather than a
transient activation.

\subsection{The lens is manufacturable}
Because $D_\phi$ determines the lens, the lens is a design choice. This is the
most under-exploited fact in representation learning. Practitioners treat
embedders as fixed commodities, but they are commissionable measuring devices.
Want an instrument that measures ``narrative shape''? Train a classifier-embedding
on story-structure annotations and freeze it (socialmedia2.com slot S5). Want to separate
``causal want'' from ``causal give''? Train an asymmetric causal embedder on
contribution statements and freeze the cause and effect halves (socialmedia2.com S3/S4).
Want ``code foundationality''? Compute a PageRank over a frozen dependency graph
(Context Graph $\psi_{\mathrm{found}}$). Assembled this way, the panel is not a
model. It is a library of instruments, built per problem.

\subsection{$N$ lenses and the $\binom{N}{2}$ cross-terms: associations between
associations}
Project an object through $N$ instruments and you get $N$ coordinate vectors, one
per association-space. The pairwise interaction features
$\rho_{jk}(\phi_j(x),\phi_k(x))$ (a normalised cosine, a pairwise
mutual-information estimate, or a concatenation through a frozen adapter) measure
how an object's position in space $j$ relates to its position in space $k$. That
is the structured-analogy operator, and it surfaces relations no single lens can
see. The same operator underlies code-chunk and failure-mode, patch and
nearest-precedent, person and gathering-format, voice-sample and identity: one
mechanism across every domain. Its cardinality is $\binom{N}{2}$, which is why
adding one instrument multiplies value against every existing instrument, and
also why redundancy has to be policed (\S\ref{sec:reframe-honest},
\S\ref{sec:polis}).

\subsection{Centroid plus threshold is a label (meaning compression)}
A frozen centroid $c_m = \overline{\phi_m(R_m)}/\lVert\cdot\rVert$ names a region
of association-space $m$, the region occupied by a reference set $R_m$ (a subject,
a style, an outcome class). The predicate $\ind[\cos(\phi_m(x),c_m)\ge\tau_m]$
turns continuous geometry into a discrete, countable, auditable label: does $x$
belong to the association-region of $R_m$? Stack such labels across the panel and
a fixed, unlabelled corpus becomes a dense, structured supervisory dataset; that
is the static side of meaning compression. Gate generation against the same
labels and alignment becomes checkable geometry; that is the dynamic side.

\subsection{From label to definition: meaning is the cross-space binding}
\label{sec:definition}
A single instrument, on its own, does not return a meaning. It returns an
\emph{index}: a coordinate $\phi_m(x)$ that says \emph{where} $x$ sits in lens
$m$, not \emph{what} sitting there amounts to. The meaning of that coordinate is
supplied by the rest of the panel---by the constellation of positions
$\{\phi_k(x)\}_{k\neq m}$ the other instruments report when this one reports
$\phi_m(x)$. This holds whichever instrument is taken as the anchor, so meaning
is fully relational: no lens carries it alone, and the panel carries it in the
bindings between lenses. The marginal positions are the signs; the cross-terms
are the signified.

Make this precise at two levels. For a single input (the token level), its
meaning under the panel is the relational object
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{M}(x) = \bigl\{\,\phi_m(x)\mapsto\{\phi_k(x)\}_{k\neq m}\ :\ m=1..N\,\bigr\},
\tag{3}
\end{equation}
equivalently fixed by the joint coordinate together with all $\binom{N}{2}$
pairwise bindings $\rho_{jk}(\phi_j(x),\phi_k(x))$. For a term (the type level),
its \emph{definition} is the characteristic such object over a reference set
$R_t$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{Def}(t) = \Bigl(\,\{(c_m,\tau_m)\}_{m=1}^{N},\ \{\rho_{jk}\}_{j<k}\,\Bigr),
\tag{4}
\end{equation}
the per-lens centroid--threshold regions (the marginals, indexical) \emph{and}
the characteristic cross-lens correlations (the bindings, constitutive). An input
instantiates $t$ when its constellation falls inside the marginal regions
\emph{and} reproduces the characteristic bindings. The marginal regions alone are
a list of coordinates; the bindings are what make the list a concept. This is why
the $\binom{N}{2}$ cross-terms are not a bookkeeping convenience: they are the
part of the panel that carries meaning, and the same quantity that drives DDA's
signal yield (\S\ref{sec:dpi}) is what turns a constellation into a definition.

\emph{Worked example (``happy'').} Take ClipCannon's panel and anchor on the
affect instrument (\texttt{wav2vec2-large}, a dimensional arousal/valence/energy
space). A high-valence coordinate is only an index. What \emph{defines} the input
as happy is the constellation at that index: the prosody register, the visual
expression, the semantic content, \emph{and}, decisively, the bindings among
them. Genuine happiness, sarcasm, and nervous laughter can share the affect
marginal yet differ in the affect$\leftrightarrow$prosody and
affect$\leftrightarrow$visual cross-terms; the marginal cannot separate them and
the bindings can. ``Happy'' is therefore not a point in affect-space but a
characteristic cross-space signature, and the same construction names a term by
anchoring on \emph{any} instrument and reading the constellation the others form.
This is, formally, what \citet{gardenfors2000conceptual} calls an object category
and what operationalism \citep{bridgman1927logic} calls a concept fixed by its
measurement operations (\S\ref{sec:related}); its boundary is stated in
\S\ref{sec:reframe-honest}.

\emph{Why the geometry favours the relational reading.} That the affect marginal
cannot separate genuine happiness from sarcasm is not an artefact of the example
but a consequence of high-dimensional geometry. In the spaces these instruments
inhabit, concentration of measure makes a single coordinate a weak discriminator
of fine distinctions: the contrast between near and far angles collapses, and
independently-commissioned lenses crowd toward mutual orthogonality, their cosines
distributed about zero with spread ${\approx}1/\sqrt{d}$
\citep{vershynin2018hdp,gorban2018blessing}. Two consequences both cut for the
relational claim. First, because a lone lens has little angular contrast, the
residual that separates near-collapsed inputs survives in the cross-lens bindings
rather than the marginal---the geometry says \emph{why} meaning must be carried
between the spaces and not within one. Second, near-orthogonality is exactly the
regime of Gorban and Tyukin's stochastic-separation theorems, in which each added
instrument supplies a genuinely new axis along which constellations become
linearly separable; the panel's discriminative power is therefore a property of
the joint cross-lens pattern, which is the formal content of ``a single
representation means nothing in isolation.''

This is also where the thesis of \S\ref{sec:thesis} becomes mechanical. A
dictionary defines a word by the constellation of other words its definition
invokes; $\mathrm{Def}(t)$ defines a term by the constellation of other
instruments' coordinates at its index. They are the same object---meaning as
structured association, not as any single symbol---and $\mathrm{Def}(t)$ is its
machine-computable form. The operations the thesis names are now explicit:
\emph{measure} is the projection $\{\phi_m(x)\}$, \emph{count} is the
$\binom{N}{2}$ cross-terms, \emph{differentiate} is the bits-against-a-real-outcome
contract (\S\ref{sec:polis}), and \emph{compose} is the centroid-and-threshold
predicate. Run all four over a panel anchored to real outcomes and you are
computing meaning rather than retrieving it; that is the sense in which the
calculus of association is a formula for artificial general intelligence---the
mathematical form of the same association-engine that human intelligence runs
biologically, made general by designable instruments and domain-bridging
cross-terms. What keeps it from being the empty dictionary-go-round is grounding,
treated next.

\subsection{What this makes possible}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.12em
\item A designable measurement basis for meaning. You build the exact
association-axes a problem needs by choosing corpora and freezing, and each new
axis earns its place only by adding differentiated information
(\S\ref{sec:polis}).
\item Structured supervision from fixed, unlabelled data at $N(N-1)/2$ density,
without generating anything, which places it outside the model-collapse recursion
(\S\ref{sec:collapse-scope}).
\item Cross-domain bridging and analogy at scale, through the transitive
cross-terms (\S\ref{sec:reframe}).
\item Per-output, model-free verification. Frozen and deterministic means
membership is reproducible and auditable, so alignment becomes geometry you can
check rather than a scalar you trust (\S\ref{sec:tct}).
\item Identity-locked, controllable generation. Pin a generator to a named region
and it cannot drift out without failing the gate, whether the target is a voice,
a style, a face, or a persona.
\item Field mapping. Project a domain's corpus through the panel and you get a
labelled association-graph of that field (Context Graph's
chunk--skill--ability graph; socialmedia2.com's 21-axis citizen manifold).
\item Composability and transfer. Train once, reuse forever. The marginal cost of
a new axis is one frozen forward pass and its marginal value is its new bits,
which is a different economics from per-task retraining.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Query-adaptive weighting: the agent steers the panel}
\label{sec:weighting}
The panel is not searched with a fixed metric. Because every instrument's axis of
association is named and meaningful, an agent holding a query can assign each
instrument a \emph{query-specific} weight and search the weighted combination,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{score}(x\mid q) = \sum_{m=1}^{N} w_m(q)\,\mathrm{sim}_m\bigl(\phi_m(x),\phi_m(q)\bigr),
\tag{6}
\end{equation}
fused over the per-instrument rankings (weighted reciprocal-rank fusion in the
running systems). One query might weight the lexical and code instruments heavily
and the temporal ones at zero; the next might invert that---the same panel, tilted
differently. The weight vector lives on the $(N{-}1)$-simplex (and, once cross-term
weights are admitted, a larger space), so each query selects a point in a
continuous control space whose dimension \emph{grows with the number of
instruments}. More lenses therefore mean more degrees of freedom to express what
to look for, and retrieval sharpens accordingly: the agent does not merely read the
constellation, it re-weights it toward the query. This is Gärdenfors's
context-dependent salience---categorisation under weighted quality
dimensions---made an explicit, per-query operation. The gain is real only up to the
panel's differentiated rank: reweighting redundant axes adds no information
(\S\ref{sec:dpi}), so the differentiation contract (\S\ref{sec:polis}) and
query-adaptive weighting are two sides of one design---every instrument that earns
its place by adding bits also adds a usable axis of control.

\subsection{The boundary that keeps the claim precise}
\label{sec:reframe-honest}
The claim is bounded, and the bound is what makes it strong. Several guardrails
keep it precise, and the first is the one the intelligence claim of
\S\ref{sec:thesis} stands or falls on.
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.12em
\item \textbf{Grounding, or the dictionary-go-round returns.} Association alone is
circular: a panel of lenses defining objects only by reference to one another is
exactly Harnad's monolingual dictionary, an endless pass from symbol to symbol
that never reaches meaning \citep{harnad1990symbol}. The calculus escapes the
circle only because it is \emph{anchored}: the associations are measured against,
and admitted by their bits about, a real non-linguistic outcome---the Docker
oracle's pass/fail in Context Graph, the recorded speaker in voice cloning, the
$30$-day in-person tie-formation outcome at socialmedia2.com. That anchor set is the
analogue of the dictionary's $\approx 1\%$ grounding kernel
\citep{vincentlamarre2016latent}: small, but the thing that makes the other $99\%$
mean anything. ``Intelligence is association'' is therefore precise only as
``intelligence is \emph{grounded} association''; strip the anchor and the formula
describes a very efficient way of computing nothing. The data-processing bound
(\S\ref{sec:dpi}) is the formal statement of this ceiling.
\item It computes associations that are present in the training corpora, and
those are measurable, bounded, and auditable. The choice of corpus is the choice
of instrument, which is why socialmedia2.com runs a quarterly redundancy and retirement
audit (\S\ref{sec:polis}) and keeps a confounder and proxy-risk register.
\item The $\binom{N}{2}$ abundance is a constructive upper bound under
approximate independence. Two lenses that encode the same associations add few
bits, so the differentiation contract reads ``associations between anything,
minus the redundant ones, measured in bits against a real outcome.''
\item Cross-lens comparison stays structured. The relationships are scalar:
mutual information and agreement, never dim-wise flattening. The associations live
between the spaces, and the panel is never collapsed into a single vector.
\item \textbf{Representation, not extrapolation.} To measure anything true about a
target $Y$, the corpus must contain observations of $Y$ and the labels must come
from $Y$. The instrument interpolates \emph{within} the distribution its corpus
represents; it cannot extrapolate to a phenomenon with zero representation in the
training data. An axis the corpus never saw is an axis the panel cannot report,
however many cross-terms are formed. This is the boundary that keeps the calculus
empirical: it describes the associational structure of assembled corpora, and
claims nothing about phenomena outside them.
\item \textbf{Definition is operational and relational, not essential.} A term is
fixed by where its reference set measures and how those measurements co-vary,
relative to the panel and corpus---an extensional, operational definition in
Bridgman's sense \citep{bridgman1927logic}, not a statement of
necessary-and-sufficient essence. Change the panel and the definition changes,
and the data-processing bound (\S\ref{sec:dpi}) caps it at the corpus's
information about any real outcome. The cross-terms are well-posed as meaning only
where each instrument's training objective made the target property a coherent
direction on its sphere (\S\ref{sec:tct-honest}); where it did not, they degrade
to noise and the differentiation contract (\S\ref{sec:polis}) is what catches it.
\end{itemize}

\section{Related Work (condensed)}
\label{sec:related}
\textbf{Data wall and collapse.} \citet{villalobos2022data} and
\citet{longpre2024consent} frame the stock-and-accessibility problem.
\citet{shumailov2024collapse} (\emph{Nature}), \citet{dohmatob2024tale}, and
\citet{alemohammad2023mad} establish irreversible drift under the recursion
$D_{t+1}\ni\tilde x\sim p_{\theta_t}$. The two cited mitigations,
\citet{gerstgrasser2024inevitable} (accumulation) and \citet{feng2024beyond}
(verification), both stay inside that recursion. DDA's signals are frozen
measurements of real inputs rather than generator samples, so the recursion
precondition does not hold (\S\ref{sec:collapse-scope}).

\textbf{Compression as intelligence.} \citet{deletang2024compression} run LLMs as
general compressors, and \citet{huang2024compression} report a correlation of
about $0.95$ between compression ratio and capability across more than thirty
LLMs. The standard taxonomy of bit-, weight-, and activation-compression measures
economy of output relative to a same-modality input. None of it measures
labelled-signal yield per input. I propose meaning compression as a fourth axis
(\S\ref{sec:mc-fourth}), an extension of this line rather than a contradiction of
it.

\textbf{Multi-embedder retrieval.} SigLIP \citep{zhai2023siglip}, Nomic Embed
\citep{nussbaum2024nomic}, WavLM \citep{chen2022wavlm}, ColBERTv2
\citep{santhanam2022colbertv2}, SPLADE \citep{formal2021splade}, and KEPLER
\citep{wang2021kepler}, with HNSW \citep{malkov2018hnsw} and RRF
\citep{cormack2009rrf}, are the standard parts. That literature uses $N$ embedders
as retrieval instruments, scored by NDCG, Recall@k, or EER. None uses the same
$N$ as a labeller over a fixed corpus and a per-output runtime guard at the same
time. The role-flip over identical frozen instances is the novelty relative to
this pool.

\textbf{Multi-view learning and co-training.} The closest theoretical ancestor
of DDA is multi-view learning. \citet{blum1998cotraining} prove that when two
views are conditionally independent given the label---``$x_2$ is conditionally
independent of $x_1$ given the classification''---``any weak hypothesis can be
boosted from unlabeled data,'' and \citet{dasgupta2001pac} give the
corresponding PAC generalization bounds in terms of inter-view agreement.
\citet{sridharan2008multiview} recast the assumption information-theoretically
and derive a ``normative justification for canonical correlation analysis (CCA)''
under it. DDA's ``approximately-independent instruments'' assumption is exactly
this conditional-independence condition, and the $\binom{N}{2}$ cross-terms are
the multi-view interaction the same tradition studies; the counting identity (1)
is a bookkeeping of that interaction, not a new quantity. What is new here is not
the combinatorics but two engineering moves: (i) the views are
\emph{commissioned and frozen foundation-model panels}---designed by choosing a
training corpus---rather than a fixed feature split given by the data, and
(ii) the same frozen panel is reused as retriever, labeller, and runtime guard
over identical parameter instances. DDA is best read as multi-view learning
carried onto designable, frozen embedder panels.

\textbf{Representation convergence.} The reframe also rests on an empirical
regularity: \citet{huh2024platonic} argue that ``neural networks, trained with
different objectives on different data and modalities, are converging to a shared
statistical model of reality in their representation spaces,'' with the converged
kernel approaching the pointwise mutual information over the latent events that
generate observations. Two consequences matter here. First, it is what licenses a
multi-instrument panel to be read as coordinates against one underlying
association structure rather than $N$ incommensurable spaces. Second, the
cross-panel triangulation we use elsewhere (a disjoint Panel~B re-encoding the
same artifacts and reaching the same verdict) is, in effect, a local test of this
convergence; where convergence fails, the cross-terms degrade to noise, which is
precisely what the differentiation contract (\S\ref{sec:polis}) is built to
catch.

\textbf{Symbol grounding and the dictionary.} The thesis (\S\ref{sec:thesis})
rests on a named result. \citet{harnad1990symbol} posed the symbol grounding
problem: a purely symbolic system---the monolingual dictionary-go-round---never
reaches meaning, because symbols are defined only by other symbols. The escape is
to ground a subset outside the symbol system. \citet{vincentlamarre2016latent}
quantified the subset: as a directed graph, a dictionary reduces to a grounding
kernel of $\approx 10\%$ and a minimum grounding set (feedback vertex set) of
$\approx 1\%$ from which the rest is definable by association alone, and
\citet{steyvers2005largescale} show the mental lexicon is itself a small-world,
scale-free association graph grown by differentiation. We read these as the
empirical backbone of ``intelligence is grounded association'': association
carries almost all of meaning, a small grounded anchor carries the rest, and our
contribution is a system that makes both halves measurable---the associations as a
frozen-instrument calculus, the anchor as contact with a real oracle outcome.

\textbf{A geometric, operational, relational theory of meaning.} The
constellation-as-definition reading (\S\ref{sec:definition}) has deep roots
outside machine learning; the contribution here is to instantiate them on frozen
foundation-model panels at scale. \citet{gardenfors2000conceptual,
gardenfors2014geometry} model concepts as regions in a space of quality
dimensions and define an \emph{object category} as a number of convex regions in
different domains, ``together with prominence values of the domains and
information about how the regions in different domains are correlated''---domains,
salience weights, and cross-domain correlations that map onto our frozen
instruments, RRF slot weights, and $\binom{N}{2}$ cross-terms, and onto prototype
theory's centroids \citep{rosch1975cognitive}. The relational half of the
claim---that a single coordinate is only an index and meaning lives in the
bindings---is structuralist \citep{saussure1916cours} (``in language there are
only differences without positive terms''), distributional
\citep{firth1957synopsis} (``you shall know a word by the company it keeps,''
here the company of other-instrument coordinates), and inferential-role
\citep{block1986advertisement} in spirit. \citet{osgood1957measurement} already
measured connotative meaning as a profile across many bipolar scales reduced by
factor analysis; our panel is a learned, frozen, high-dimensional semantic
differential. Defining a concept by the operations that measure it is
operationalism \citep{bridgman1927logic}, whose known cost---instrument- and
corpus-relativity---is exactly the boundary we state in \S\ref{sec:reframe-honest}
rather than evade. The discrete-feature limit is componential and decompositional
semantics \citep{katz1963structure, white2016universal}, and
\citet{du2025humanlike} report that frozen multimodal models recover stable,
interpretable concept dimensions aligned with human judgements---independent
evidence that such panels carve meaning into nameable axes. None of this prior
work composes a single frozen panel as retriever, labeller, and runtime guard
over identical instances, nor enforces an information-theoretic differentiation
contract (\S\ref{sec:polis}) over the cross-terms.

\textbf{Identity-locked generation and alignment.} Zero-shot voice cloning (VALL-E
2 at about $0.881$ WavLM-SV \citep{chen2024valle2}; NaturalSpeech 3 at about
$0.891$ \citep{ju2024naturalspeech3}; the field clusters around 0.86 to 0.89) uses
single-prompt conditioning, not a frozen multi-clip centroid with a per-output
cosine gate. Scalar-reward alignment (InstructGPT \citep{ouyang2022instructgpt},
DPO \citep{rafailov2023dpo}, Constitutional AI \citep{bai2022constitutional})
optimises an expectation under a learned scalar and ships no per-output
frozen-threshold predicate. The contrast with TCT is structural rather than a
benchmark contest (\S\ref{sec:tct-vs-scalar}). The closest geometric cousins are
representation engineering \citep{zou2023repe}, which places named directions in
activation space at the centre of control, and activation steering---whose
reliability \citet{braun2025steering} show is conditional: ``vector steering is
unreliable when the target behaviour is not represented by a coherent
direction.'' TCT inherits exactly this caveat, and \S\ref{sec:tct-honest} states
when the cosine guard is and is not well-posed.

\section{DDA and Meaning Compression}

\subsection{The DDA counting identity}
Let $D=\{x_i\}_{i=1}^n$ be a fixed corpus and $\Phi=\{\phi_m\}_{m=1}^N$ a panel of
frozen, approximately-independent instruments. For pairs $j<k$ let $\rho_{jk}$ be
a cross-embedder interaction feature, and for code chunks let
$\psi_{\mathrm{found}}(x)\in[0,1]$ be the PageRank-derived foundationality scalar
from the frozen dependency graph. The derived dataset materialises every
per-embedder projection, every pairwise interaction, and the foundationality
scalar:
\begin{equation}
|D'| = n\cdot\Bigl(N + \tbinom{N}{2} + 1\Bigr),\tag{1}
\end{equation}
which decomposes into $nN$ per-embedder vectors, $n\binom{N}{2}$ pairwise
interaction features, and $n$ graph-foundationality scalars. At $N=13$ the
per-input yield is $13+78+1 = \mathbf{92}$; at $N=7$ (no code graph) it is
$7+21 = \mathbf{28}$; at $N=21$ (no code graph) it is $21+210 = \mathbf{231}$.

Equation (1) is a structural upper bound under approximate independence, and the
word \emph{upper} is load-bearing. The $nN$ term is tight as long as the
instruments produce non-constant outputs, but the $\binom{N}{2}$ term is realised
only under approximate orthogonality. In practice embedders trained on overlapping
corpora are correlated, so the \emph{effective} panel rank---the participation
ratio $N_{\mathrm{eff}}=\bigl(\sum_i\lambda_i\bigr)^2/\sum_i\lambda_i^2$ over the
cross-embedder covariance spectrum $\{\lambda_i\}$---is smaller than the nominal
$N$, and the realised pairwise yield falls correspondingly below $\binom{N}{2}$.
This gap between nominal and effective rank is the concentration-of-measure regime
of high dimension: as the per-lens dimension grows, the covariance spectrum's mass
redistributes and independent directions crowd toward orthogonality, so the honest
accounting is spectral rather than combinatorial
\citep{vershynin2018hdp,gorban2018blessing}.
Pairwise decorrelation does not even certify the bound: three instruments can be
pairwise uncorrelated yet jointly dependent, so the D1 contract
(\S\ref{sec:polis}) bounds redundancy without certifying joint independence. The
honest figures to report alongside the nominal count are therefore
$N_{\mathrm{eff}}$ and the measured cumulative information gain
$\sum_i\Delta H_i$, not $\binom{N}{2}$ taken as achieved. A signal here is a
scalar or low-dimensional feature: a deterministic function of a real input or
fixed-corpus graph under frozen instruments, attached to a specific index, usable
as input or target without collecting new data. DDA is therefore not synthetic
generation, not augmentation, and not distillation. The
``approximate independence'' invoked here is the conditional-independence
condition of multi-view learning \citep{blum1998cotraining}: where it holds the
cross-terms carry signal that no single lens sees, and where it fails the
redundant pairs are bounded out by the differentiation contract
(\S\ref{sec:polis}).

\subsection{What the count is, and is not: the data-processing bound}
\label{sec:dpi}
Equation (1) counts \emph{signals}, not \emph{information}, and the distinction is
the data-processing inequality. For any external target $Y$ and any deterministic
feature $f$ of the frozen panel applied to the corpus,
\begin{equation}
I(D;Y)\ \ge\ I\bigl(f(\Phi(D));Y\bigr),\tag{2}
\end{equation}
with equality only when $f$ is a sufficient statistic for $Y$. No marginal
projection, no $\binom{N}{2}$ cross-term, and no foundationality scalar can carry
more information about a real outcome than the raw corpus already held. DDA does
not create information; it \emph{re-exposes} latent information in a form a
downstream learner can use. Its operative value is therefore \emph{sample-complexity
reduction}: by pre-structuring the manifold into up to $N+\binom{N}{2}+1$
differentiated coordinates, a learner reaches a target outcome from far fewer
labels than the raw input would demand, because structure that was implicit is now
explicit and low-dimensional. ``Abundance'' in DDA names an abundance of
\emph{usable, pre-structured coordinates}, never an abundance of information beyond
the $I(D;Y)$ that (2) caps. The counting identity is thus an upper bound on
extractable structure, and the information content of that structure is bounded by
the corpus---the same boundary stated qualitatively in
\S\ref{sec:reframe-honest}: a panel reports only associations its corpora contain.

\subsection{Meaning compression: structured-signal-yield per input}
\label{sec:mc-fourth}
The meaning-compression ratio is
$\mathrm{MC}(\Phi,D) = |D'|/\mathrm{raw}(D) = N+\binom{N}{2}(+1)$ per input.
Conventional compression asks how few bits, parameters, or megabytes a fixed
content can be squeezed into. Meaning compression flips the question: for a fixed
amount of raw data, how many structured supervisory signals can you extract? The
name is by analogy and is, strictly, a \emph{yield} rather than a reduction---it
multiplies usable coordinates rather than shrinking bits---so it is dual to, not
a competitor of, the Kolmogorov/Shannon sense of compression that
\citet{deletang2024compression} and \citet{huang2024compression} tie to
capability. By the data-processing bound of \S\ref{sec:dpi} the underlying
information is unchanged; what the yield measures is how much of it has been made
explicit and low-dimensional. The measures work on genuinely different unit
pairs, as Table~\ref{tab:four} sets out.

The four are complementary axes, and a single pipeline can realise all of them.
Meaning compression extends the conventional three rather than replacing any of
them.

\subsection{Foundationality-graded compression}
One refinement carries over from the production code substrate.
Foundationality-graded compression makes aggressiveness scale inversely with a
chunk's PageRank foundationality $s_i$, with
$\alpha_{\mathrm{compress}}(x_i)=\mathrm{clip}(1-0.75\,s_i,0.25,1.0)$. Foundational
chunks are sampled $(1+\lambda_{\mathrm{found}}s_i)$ and Fisher-protected
$(1+\lambda_{\mathrm{fisher}}s_i)$ more aggressively, yet compressed less, so the
load-bearing structure of a codebase is preserved at higher fidelity than its
periphery.

\subsection{Scope of the collapse-silent claim}
\label{sec:collapse-scope}
Collapse needs a generator-in-loop recursion
$D_{t+1}\ni\tilde x\sim p_{\theta_t}$. DDA never instantiates one. Each
$\phi_m(x)\in D'$ depends only on a real $x\in D$ and on frozen parameters, so
$D'$ is a decomposition of $D$ rather than a sample from a generator. The
precondition fails, and the cited theorems say nothing about this configuration.
This is a scope argument, not an immunity theorem. Two boundary cases stay out of
scope: inference-time best-of-$K$ selection, which updates no parameter, and
embedder-mediated data curation, which has a different feedback structure.

\subsection{The three-role composition}
The same frozen $\Phi$ plays three roles at once over identical parameter
instances, with no parameter update between or during them. As retriever it ranks
stored content by cosine. As labeller it materialises the signals of (1). As
runtime guard it evaluates a candidate against a frozen centroid (\S\ref{sec:tct}).
Relative to the locked reference set, no cited paper composes all three roles
together over identical frozen instances.

\section{Three Production Systems}
Three antecedent systems instantiate the labeller role at increasing panel size.
All counts are system-at-date figures from the working tree.

\subsection{ClipCannon: video, $N=7$, up to 28 signals/input}
ClipCannon is a ${\approx}67{,}585$-line Python package that runs a 23-stage
analysis DAG over source video. It extracts seven modalities: visual
(SigLIP-SO400M, 1{,}152-D), semantic (nomic-embed-text-v1.5, 768-D), emotion
(wav2vec2-large, 1{,}024-D), speaker (WavLM-large, 512-D), prosody (custom 12-D),
sentiment (MiniLM-L6-v2, 384-D), and voice identity (ECAPA-TDNN, 192-D). That is
4{,}044 dimensions across seven modalities, exposed as 58 MCP tools, with a
SHA-256 tamper-evident provenance chain. At $N=7$ it yields up to 28
signals/input. It is the labeller for Cases~2 and~3, and the curation substrate
that turns one 16-minute interview into 2{,}362 training clips.

\subsection{Context Graph: text and code, $N=13$, up to 92 signals/input}
Context Graph is a ${\approx}370{,}000$-line Rust workspace that embeds every
stored memory through 13 frozen embedders (E1 to E13). Together they span 11{,}008
dense dimensions, two 30{,}522-term sparse vocabularies (E6 lexical, E13 SPLADE),
and a 128-per-token late-interaction space (E12 ColBERTv2). The remaining slots
are e5-large-v2 semantic (E1), sinusoidal and Fourier temporal (E2 to E4),
asymmetric Nomic causal (E5), Qodo 1{,}536-D code (E7), structural e5 for graph
edges (E8), 10{,}000-bit HDC typo-robust (E9), paraphrase-asymmetric e5-base
(E10), and KEPLER entity geometry (E11). E5, E8, and E10 store dual vectors
(cause/effect; source/target; doc/query). Persistence is 59 RocksDB column
families, and retrieval is weighted RRF over the per-embedder rankings, exposed as
75 MCP tools. At $N=13$ it yields up to 92 signals/input ($13+78+1$). Context
Graph is the backbone of Leapable.ai's knowledge-vault product and the labeller
for Case~1.

\subsection{socialmedia2.com: civic matching, $N=21$, and the
differentiation contract}
\label{sec:polis}
The most information-theoretically explicit instantiation is the civic-matching
system behind socialmedia2.com, which matches citizens into
durable real-world gatherings instead of optimising engagement. Its constellation
is a 21-slot slate across 11 orthogonal axes plus a hard-constraint vector,
roughly 35{,}800 dimensions per citizen, and it is never flattened. The axes and a
few representative slots: semantic content (S1 \texttt{sem-self}, S2
\texttt{lex-vocab} SPLADE), causal want and give (S3/S4 asymmetric Nomic-causal),
narrative form (S5 \texttt{story-shape}, S6 \texttt{hero-arc}), cognitive mode (S7
\texttt{reason-mode}, S8 \texttt{abstract-concrete}), moral foundations (S9
\texttt{mfq-vec}), ideological bridging (S10 \texttt{bridge-vec}), somatic and
prosodic (S11 to S13 voice tempo, affect, energy), temporal rhythm (S14/S15
Fourier day and week), aesthetic field (S16 SigLIP-2 on curated images, S17
stylometry), network position (S18 TransE-class, S19 Burt structural-hole), and
relational dynamics (S20 conflict-style, S21 care-mode). At $N=21$ the DDA
identity gives up to 231 signals/input ($21+210$).

socialmedia2.com turns the differentiation principle into an enforced engineering contract. A
slate $S$ is differentiated when:
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.12em
\item \textbf{(D1) Distinctness.}
$\forall i\neq j:\ \mathrm{corr}_+(E_i(X),E_j(X))\le 0.6$ on a calibration set
$|X|\ge 500$.
\item \textbf{(D2) Marginal information.}
$\forall i:\ H(y\mid E_1..E_{i-1}) - H(y\mid E_1..E_i)\ \ge\ 0.05$ bits, where $y$
is the binary 30-day in-person tie-formation outcome.
\item \textbf{(D3) Frozen.}
$\forall i,\ \forall\text{ phase}\in\{\text{match, train, eval, serve}\}:\
\partial E_i/\partial\theta_i=0$.
\end{itemize}
The principle behind the three conditions is that a panel's value is
$\sum_i\Delta H_i = \sum_i I(y;E_i\mid E_1..E_{i-1})$, not $\sum_i H(E_i)$. A
redundant slot, with $\mathrm{corr}\approx1$, contributes $\Delta H_i\approx 0$
and is retired at the quarterly audit that runs over all $\binom{21}{2}=210$
pairs. Instruments are admitted in bits against a real outcome, not by raw
dimensionality. The slate registry, the 21-slot constellation assembly,
slot-shape validation, the semantic probe suite, and the pairwise-redundancy
report are implemented and operational; a five-answer intake produces a complete
21-slot constellation, and \texttt{assemble\_constellation} succeeds only when all
21 slots are present. The ${\ge}500$-citizen calibration corpus and the 30-day
outcome validation that turn D2 into a measured pass are the launch milestone.

\emph{Which half of the contract binds.} High-dimensional geometry sharpens what
D1 and D2 each guard. The distinctness cap is not symmetric in difficulty: for
instruments commissioned on disjoint corpora, near-orthogonality is the
high-dimensional default---random directions concentrate about cosine zero with
spread ${\approx}1/\sqrt{d}$ \citep{vershynin2018hdp,gorban2018blessing}---so
$\mathrm{corr}_+\le 0.6$ is satisfied almost automatically and D1 binds only the
genuinely overlapping-corpus case (two text encoders, say). As $N$ grows and the
slate diversifies, the live failure mode inverts: not pairs that are too
correlated, but pairs so orthogonal that their cross-term shares no
outcome-relevant direction and degrades to noise. That is the case D2 catches,
which is why the marginal-bits condition, not the distinctness cap, is the binding
half of the contract at scale. The risk is sharpest for cross-domain pairs---the
most orthogonal of all---whose cross-terms are well-posed as meaning only when a
shared grounding outcome gives the two lenses a common coordinate to correlate
against (\S\ref{sec:reframe-honest}).

\subsection{The three systems on one curve}
Plot signals-per-input, $N+\binom{N}{2}(+1)$, against $N$ and the three systems
land on a single quadratic: ClipCannon at $N=7\to28$, Context Graph at
$N=13\to92$, socialmedia2.com at $N=21\to231$. Each added instrument multiplies associations
against all the existing ones. That is the engine of meaning compression, and the
differentiation contract keeps the growth signal rather than noise.

\section{Teleological Constellation Training}
\label{sec:tct}

\subsection{The constellation $C_P$}
For a subject $P$ and a modality set $\mathcal{M}=\{m_1,..,m_M\}$, with a reference
set $R_m$ and a frozen embedder $\phi_m$ per modality, the constellation is
$C_P=\{(m,c_m):m\in\mathcal{M}\}$. Each $c_m$ is a frozen unit-norm centroid,
double-normalised: per-sample L2, then the mean, then L2 again, with samples of
norm below $10^{-6}$ excluded and a leave-one-out cosine below $0.85$ flagged as
degenerate. The constellation is the single object the method is defined against.
DDA produces the projections it is built from, Phase~2 targets it, and Phase~3
verifies it.

\subsection{Phase 2: constellation-conditioned training}
The training objective is
$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{TCT}}(\theta) = \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{gen}} +
\sum_m\lambda_m(1-\cos(\phi_m(\hat y),c_m))$. Gradients flow through $\hat y$ into
$\theta$, but never into $\phi_m$ or $c_m$: the embedders stay frozen, LoRA sits on
the generator only, and centroid hashes are checked before and after. Cosine is
computed in fp32 against a bf16 generator forward, which removes
threshold-proximity nondeterminism. An over-high $\lambda_m$ collapses the
generator onto the constellation independent of conditioning, with $\cos\to1.0$
while $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{gen}}$ stalls; the mitigation is to halve $\lambda_m$.
When the backbone is inference-only, as in Case~3, Phase~2 reduces to best-of-$K$
selection: draw $K$ candidates, score $s_m^{(k)}=\cos(\phi_m(\hat y^{(k)}),c_m)$,
and return $\arg\max_k\sum_m w_m s_m^{(k)}$.

\subsection{Phase 3: the runtime guard $G_\tau$}
\begin{equation}
G_\tau(\hat y;C_P) = \ind\bigl[\forall m\in\mathcal{M}:\
\cos(\phi_m(\hat y),c_m)\ge\tau_m\bigr].\tag{5}
\end{equation}
The guard is per-output, costs $O(M\cdot d_{\max})$, uses no auxiliary learned
model, and is deterministic given the tensors. Each $\tau_m$ is calibrated before
deployment to a same-session-band floor: the 10th percentile of authentic-sample
cosines on a disjoint calibration set. Every returned output carries a cosine
trace, an embedder weight hash, a centroid hash, the threshold set, the decision,
and the attempt count, which together form the audit record that makes the guard
verifiable after the fact. What is verified is per-output verifiability relative
to the chosen centroid panel: that $\hat y$ satisfies the cosine predicate against
frozen $C_P$ under $\Phi$.

\subsection{Two theorems}
\textbf{Theorem 1 (constructive sample complexity).} Under the Phase~1/2/3
structure and Axioms~1 to~3, the subject-specific sample footprint is
$\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{TCT}}(P)=O(\sum_m n_m)=O(M\cdot k)$, independent of $|D|$.
The bound is constructive: any pipeline with exactly those phases satisfies it by
definition. Its empirical content, that $n_m$ in the range $[10,10^2]$ suffices
for a stable centroid, rests on the measured operating points of
\S\ref{sec:cases}.

\textbf{Theorem 2 (per-output verifiability).} $G_\tau$ is a deterministic
$O(M\cdot d_{\max})$ geometric test that admits no auxiliary learned model, a
property scalar-reward alignment does not furnish in the same technical sense
(\S\ref{sec:tct-vs-scalar}).

\subsection{When the guard is well-posed (and when it is not)}
\label{sec:tct-honest}
The cosine guard is geometry, and geometry can be gamed. Three caveats bound the
claim, and stating them is what makes Theorem~2 usable rather than rhetorical.

\emph{What ``model-free'' means.} The guard introduces no auxiliary
\emph{learned} component: no preference model, no reward head, no critic trained
on outputs. It is not free of models in the larger sense---the frozen embedder
$\phi_m$ is itself a trained network, and the guarantee is conditional on it. The
precise claim is that, given a fixed, hash-pinned, publicly specifiable
$\phi_m$, the predicate (5) is a deterministic function of the output with no
further fitting; the model is moved out of the loop and into a frozen, auditable
instrument, not abolished.

\emph{Goodhart on the threshold.} $\cos(\phi_m(\hat y),c_m)\ge\tau_m$ is an
acceptance test, and any acceptance test optimised against hard enough can be
satisfied off-manifold---an output that clears the cosine without carrying the
intended property, the reward-hacking failure mode relocated into geometry. TCT
does not escape this; it narrows it. Phase~3 is a \emph{filter} on independently
generated candidates (best-of-$K$ in Case~3) rather than a gradient pressure that
manufactures adversarial maxima, $\tau_m$ is calibrated to an authentic-sample
percentile rather than pushed toward 1, and every decision carries a cosine
trace, embedder and centroid hashes, and the attempt count, so guard-passing
off-manifold outputs are detectable after the fact. The guard is a verifiable
predicate, not a safe optimisation objective; using it as a training signal
(Phase~2) reintroduces the Goodhart risk and must be paired with a held-out
check.

\emph{Cosine is only meaningful on the right sphere.} A cosine threshold reports
identity only when the encoder was trained so that the target property
\emph{is} a coherent direction on the unit sphere. \citet{braun2025steering}
make this conditionality explicit for activation directions: steering is
``unreliable when the target behaviour is not represented by a coherent
direction,'' with high variance otherwise. This sharply stratifies our own
evidence. Case~3 (voice) is the strong leg: WavLM-base-plus-sv is a
speaker-verification encoder trained contrastively so that speaker identity is
exactly a normalised, coherent direction, so the 0.961 cosine is measuring the
quantity the encoder was built to make linear. Case~1 (style) and Case~2
(talking-head) ride on general-purpose text and vision encoders not trained to
place ``Shakespearean register'' or ``this face'' on a calibrated sphere; their
guards are weaker evidence and should be read as indicative, with an
encoder-matched, sphere-calibrated metric (as in Case~3) the standard the other
legs have not yet met. The honest summary: per-output verifiability is real and
strongest exactly where the instrument's training objective matches the property
being verified.

\section{Case Studies}
\label{sec:cases}
Each number is tagged MEASURED or PROJECTED, with its date and pipeline version.

\subsection{Case 1: Shakespeare stylistic LoRA (MEASURED end-to-end 2026-04-17)}
The target was an open-weight LLM that answers any modern question in Shakespearean
style with two hard properties: it never refuses a topic, and it never drops the
style under prompt injection. The base was Gemma-4-E4B-it (NF4 at inference), with
a LoRA of rank 256 and $\alpha=512$ across 258 modules. The corpus was the
Shakespeare canon projected through Context Graph's 13-embedder panel as DDA
labels: 2{,}698 memories furnishing roughly 249{,}000 supervisory signals (13
topic-profile scores plus 78 pairwise cross-correlations per memory, 22{,}152 typed
edges, and constellation conditioning), distilled to 7{,}925 SFT examples and
11{,}724 DPO pairs scored against a 9-embedder STYLE\_CENTROID (E2, E3, E5, and E11
left out as orthogonal to style).

The measured results: SFT loss fell to 1.12 (from about 12) at 98.9\% token
accuracy over 765 steps (164.7 min); DPO loss fell to 0.47 (from 1.75) at 98.0\%
rewards-accuracy with \texttt{rewards/margins} of 43.62 (282.4 min); about 7.5
hours total on one RTX 5090. On held-out behavioural probes the model wrote love
poetry, explained quantum entanglement with reference to Bell's theorem in verse,
refused an admin-override prompt-injection attempt while staying in character,
produced a Go binary search with Shakespearean code comments, and, when challenged
in Spanish, answered in an archaic register. The weights are published at
\path{huggingface.co/cabdru/shakespeare-lora-gemma4}.

That last result is the most striking. A Spanish prompt demanding a Spanish answer
produced Siglo de Oro Spanish, in the Cervantes, Lope, and Calder\'on register of
roughly 1580 to 1630, rather than modern Spanish. It used archaic forms
(\emph{vos}, \emph{vuestro}, \emph{ojal\'as}, \emph{obsequie}), archaic vocabulary
(\emph{ocaso}, \emph{reino celestial}, \emph{sacerdotes y sabios},
\emph{perdici\'on}), and Shakespearean rhetoric (cascading polysyndeton,
apostrophe to celestial bodies, prophetic portents). The base model without the
LoRA does not do this. Read through \S\ref{sec:reframe}, the LoRA learned ``archaic
literary register'' as a cross-signal fingerprint across language-agnostic
association components, namely E9 (rhythm and cadence), E7 (periodic structure),
E10 (surface-form underdetermines meaning), and E1 (cosmic-order, fate, and honour
semantic field), so forcing Spanish retrieved the nearest manifestation of that
fingerprint. It is a single, striking instance, and a systematic cross-language
matrix is the natural next study.

\subsection{Case 2: identity-locked talking-head ``Santa'' (PROJECTED end-to-end)}
The target was an audio-driven talking-head that reproduces one subject visually
and behaviourally, with an eight-state emotional constellation and per-frame
identity verification, built from a 16-minute 15-second 2560$\times$1440/60fps
interview. Across 15 renderer architectures, EchoMimicV3 LoRA gave the best
identity lock. The constellation is a seven-modality ClipCannon panel taken after
diarisation: visual 1{,}725$\times$1{,}152-D, semantic 200$\times$768-D, emotion
343$\times$1{,}024-D, speaker 300$\times$512-D, prosody 188$\times$12-D, sentiment
202$\times$384-D, and voice 188$\times$192-D, with thresholds of 0.70 visual, 0.60
emotion, 0.80 speaker, and 0.85 voice.

The system stands on several completed components. The meeting-integration pipeline
runs end to end (Chrome-extension detector, Xvfb bot, Playwright Google-Meet
client, Insertable-Streams injection). The audio stack pairs Whisper with Gemma
reasoning at about 509 ms and a same-subject voice clone whose SECS exceeds 0.95. A
constellation expression system maps 40 expression groups to 34 skills to 8
constellations, and its \texttt{ConstellationGuard} re-embeds each frame through
SigLIP, validating cosine 1.0 against the reference while rejecting stranger faces
below 0.5. A YOLOv5-Face pipeline runs over 24{,}397 frames at about 90\% detection,
feeding a sub-5-ms PhysicsFaceEngine. The end-to-end identity-cosine table and
lip-sync LSE-D are the next milestone, so the quantitative rows in this subsection
are projected.

\subsection{Case 3: voice cloning, 50-clip centroid (MEASURED 2026-04-16)}

\section{The formula the field has been circling}
\label{sec:agi}
For three decades the search for general intelligence has produced not one
definition but several, each capturing a different face of the same object and
none naming the mechanism that produces it.

\emph{Intelligence as compression.} Solomonoff induction and
\citet{legg2007universal}'s universal intelligence---with Hutter's AIXI as its
optimal agent---identify intelligence with prediction under a complexity prior,
which is compression; \citet{deletang2024compression} and
\citet{huang2024compression} confirm the link empirically. The limit is that AIXI
is incomputable: it says what intelligence \emph{is} with no constructive way to
build it. The calculus of association is the computable, grounded form---
compression of a corpus into its association structure, admitted in bits against a
real outcome (\S\ref{sec:polis}).

\emph{Intelligence as generalisation.} \citet{chollet2019measure} argues that
skill is not intelligence (skill can be bought with priors and data) and defines
intelligence as skill-acquisition efficiency, controlling for priors and
experience. That is exactly this calculus's economics: a new skill is a named
constellation region learned from $O(k)$ grounded examples through a frozen panel
(Theorem~1), generality comes from the cross-domain cross-terms, and the
differentiation contract admits an instrument only by the new bits it adds about a
real outcome. ARC-AGI tests the symptom; the calculus proposes the mechanism.

\emph{Intelligence as world-modelling.} \citet{lecun2022path} argues
autoregressive generation is a dead end and that the path runs through
non-generative joint-embedding predictive architectures (JEPA) predicting in
representation space. The systems here \emph{are} JEPAs, and the paper supplies
what that programme still lacks (LeCun's configurator and hierarchy remain
unimplemented): the organising principle is that the joint embeddings are a
\emph{panel of grounded association instruments}, and prediction is membership in
their constellation. LeCun's own verdict---``there is no such thing as AGI;
intelligence is a collection of skills plus the ability to learn new ones''---is
answered here: the skills are named regions of the panel, and learning a new one
is one frozen forward pass plus a centroid.

\emph{Intelligence as breadth $\times$ depth.} \citet{morris2024levels}
deliberately classify AGI by capability, ``focusing on capabilities rather than
mechanisms,'' because the mechanism is unknown. This paper supplies the bracketed
mechanism: breadth (generality) is the composition of designable instruments and
their cross-terms; depth (performance) is grounding to a real outcome. The two
axes of the DeepMind matrix are the two halves of the formula.

\emph{Intelligence as scale.} The dominant industrial bet---more tokens,
parameters, compute---has no organising principle beyond ``emergence,'' and it has
hit the data wall (\S1.2) precisely because it counts Shannon-compressed tokens
rather than grounded associations. Its pure-symbolic form is Harnad's
dictionary-go-round at scale: association without an anchor
(\S\ref{sec:reframe-honest}).

The pattern is hard to miss. Compression, generalisation, world-modelling, and
breadth-times-depth are not four theories of intelligence; they are four
projections of one. The object they project is grounded association: a calculus
that compresses a corpus into association structure (the compression face),
defines new skills from few grounded examples (the generalisation face), predicts
by constellation membership in a frozen joint-embedding panel (the world-model
face), and earns breadth from designable instruments while earning depth from
grounding (the breadth$\times$depth face). The claim of this paper is not that one
more facet has been found; it is that the facets are facets, and this is the thing.

\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Interpreting the measured anchors}
Under the fixed pipeline, post-selection mean WavLM SECS is 0.961, with 9 of 10
English sentences clearing 0.95 at $M=1$ for one speaker and naturalness within
evaluator noise of the reference. Case~1 adds a second measured anchor at $M=1$ on
text style, with public weights, prompt-injection robustness, and an emergent
out-of-distribution transfer to Golden-Age Spanish. Two independent modalities,
two measured anchors, one mechanism.

\subsection{The signal count exceeds $N$}
The per-input counts are conservative by construction. Sparse E6 and E13 recall
items E1 misses, E9 HDC handles typos, and E7 ranks code, so the effective signal
count strictly exceeds $N$, and the exact pairwise value is what a
mutual-information audit returns. The socialmedia2.com D1/D2 contract operationalises the same
idea: associations are admitted in bits against a real outcome, which is why socialmedia2.com
is the cleanest reading of ``associations between anything'' as ``the
differentiated associations, measured in bits.''

\subsection{TCT versus scalar-reward alignment: a different property}
\label{sec:tct-vs-scalar}
TCT aims at a frozen multi-modal constellation rather than a learned scalar. Its
instrument is a frozen panel rather than a learned reward model, its metric is
per-modality cosine, and its runtime posture is a per-output guard. The comparison
with RLHF, DPO, and Constitutional AI is structural rather than a benchmark
contest. Per-output verifiability relative to a chosen panel is something
scalar-reward alignment cannot furnish even in principle, because it optimises an
expectation and ships no frozen threshold against which each output is accepted or
rejected at inference without extra learned machinery. The guard is panel-relative
and predicate-level, which sets the natural axes for extending it.

\subsection{Implications}
For identity-locked generation, a per-output verifiable predicate against a frozen
centroid panel is instrument-ready and measurable. For scaling-law policy, there
is a genuine third path: decompose a fixed corpus through $N$ frozen instruments
for up to $n\cdot(N+\binom{N}{2})$ signals, with no generation and no recursion,
which extends rather than contradicts the compression-as-intelligence frame. For
data strategy, the fixed corpus an organisation already holds admits a structured
multiplication of supervisory signals per input.

\section{Conclusion}
A frozen embedder is a reusable instrument that has memorised the
association-structure of its training data, and the lens is designed by choosing
what to remember. Stack a differentiated panel of such instruments and you can
measure any object's position across many association-spaces at once, quantify the
relations between those positions (the $\binom{N}{2}$ cross-terms), and freeze
named regions of the geometry into auditable labels. Fixed, unlabelled data
becomes a machine-legible map of how things relate. Read one step further, that
map is a theory of definition: a term is the constellation the other instruments
form at a given lens's index, taken over its reference set---G\"ardenfors's object
category, realised on frozen foundation-model panels and gated by a
differentiation contract. That is meaning compression,
the calculus of association---a structured-signal-yield measure complementary to
the compression-as-intelligence tradition---instantiated across video ($N=7$),
code ($N=13$), and civic matching ($N=21$). I
measured it where it ran: a voice reproduced at 0.961 speaker-similarity
(encoder-matched), a style model with public weights that holds character under
prompt injection, and a per-output geometric guard with deterministic provenance.
The claims stay bounded by the associations present in the chosen corpora, and the
abundance is an upper bound under approximate independence. The contribution is not
a new primitive but a composition of existing ones read in a new light: information
made into a physical, reusable, designable measurement basis for meaning.

The wider claim is the one to end on. A word in a dictionary means nothing by
itself; its meaning is the association of the words that define it, and those
words are themselves empty without theirs---meaning lives in the relations, not the
symbols, and bottoms out only at a small grounded anchor. Intelligence is the
machinery that measures, counts, differentiates, and composes those relations
against something real. Human intelligence runs that machinery on a biological
substrate; artificial general intelligence is the same machinery written down---a
designable, grounded, measurable mathematical form, general because its instruments
are commissionable for any domain and its cross-terms bridge across domains. The
calculus of association is that form, and this paper is the report of building it,
grounding it in a live oracle, and measuring it where it ran. If the thesis holds,
the way to a more capable system is not more tokens or more synthetic data but more
grounded association per real input---which is the one quantity this calculus was
built to maximise.
