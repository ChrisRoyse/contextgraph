\noindent\textbf{Abstract.}
A trained embedder is usually treated as a black-box vectoriser. This paper
takes the opposite view. A frozen embedder is a designable measurement
instrument. It crystallises the associational structure of whatever corpus it
was trained on, so that projecting a new input through it does not so much
``encode'' the input as report where that input sits inside the corpus's whole
web of associations. The corpus is the lens. That is what makes the instrument
manufacturable: you can commission a measuring device for any axis of
association you can assemble a corpus for, freeze it, and reuse it indefinitely.

Three constructs follow from this one reframe, and each is formalised and
instantiated in a running production system. \emph{Derived Data Abundance
(DDA)} is a counting identity. A fixed corpus of $n$ real inputs projected
through $N$ frozen, approximately-independent instruments yields up to
$n\cdot(N+\binom{N}{2}+1)$ structured supervisory signals; the $\binom{N}{2}$
cross-terms are associations \emph{between} associations, a structured-analogy
operator that can relate objects sharing zero surface features. \emph{Meaning
compression} is the ratio view, structured signal per raw-data unit, which we
propose as a fourth compression category alongside bit-, weight-, and
activation-compression. \emph{Teleological Constellation Training (TCT)} reuses
the same frozen panel as a geometric alignment target, a constellation of named
identity centroids that is never flattened, with a per-output cosine guard
$G_\tau$ that gives deterministic, model-free, per-output verifiability.

Three production systems instantiate the substrate at growing panel sizes:
ClipCannon (video; $N=7$; up to 28 signals/input; ${\approx}67{,}585$-line
Python), Context Graph (text and code; $N=13$; up to 92 signals/input;
${\approx}370{,}000$-line Rust), and socialmedia2.com / Polis (civic matching;
$N=21$; up to 231 signals/input). The last enforces an explicit
information-theoretic differentiation contract: every instrument must add at
least $0.05$ bits of new information about a real outcome, and no pair may
correlate above $0.6$, or it is retired as noise. Three case studies then read
the framework back. Case~3 (voice) is measured at 0.961 mean WavLM
speaker-similarity (max 0.975; 9 of 10 at or above 0.95; $N{=}10$, one speaker)
under an encoder-matched protocol, with naturalness on par with real recordings
(DNSMOS 3.93/3.93; UTMOS 3.012/2.997) and a stated cross-encoder caveat. Case~1
(style) answers any topic, holds character under prompt injection, shows an
emergent zero-shot transfer to Golden-Age Spanish, and is published
(\path{huggingface.co/cabdru/shakespeare-lora-gemma4}). Case~2 (talking-head) is
architecturally complete with six forensic fixes but remains end-to-end
projected. We keep the boundary honest throughout. This calculus computes the
associations present in the training corpora, which are measurable, bounded, and
auditable; it does not compute ``all of reality,'' and the $\binom{N}{2}$
abundance is a constructive upper bound that still awaits a pairwise
mutual-information audit.

\section{Introduction}

\subsection{The data wall, and the two paths everyone is already on}
Frontier pre-training has nearly reached the ceiling of high-quality public
text. \citet{villalobos2022data} put the indexed human-text stock at roughly
$300$ trillion tokens and project exhaustion somewhere between 2026 and 2032,
sooner under over-training regimes. \citet{longpre2024consent} watched a quarter
of the high-quality C4 tokens become restricted in a single year. The
most-discussed response is to train on generator-synthesised data, and that road
is bounded: \citet{shumailov2024collapse} show in \emph{Nature} that recursive
self-generation drives an irreversible distributional drift they call model
collapse. The commercial consequence is a data-licensing race running to the
hundreds of millions per partnership. So the standing responses divide cleanly.
Path~1 buys more real data. Path~2 makes more synthetic data. Both count
Shannon-compressed tokens, and both stay inside the collapse-prone recursion the
moment they generate.

\subsection{The reframe: a frozen embedder is a designable lens of association}
This paper takes a third path, and it starts by asking what an embedder actually
\emph{is}. Training an embedder on a corpus and then freezing its weights does
not store the corpus. It stores the geometry of how everything in that corpus
related to everything else. The frozen weights are the associational structure of
the training data. So when a new input $x$ is projected through a frozen $\phi$,
the output $\phi(x)$ is not an encoding of $x$ at all. It is a measurement of $x$
against that whole web of associations, returning $x$'s coordinates inside it.

Three moves stack on this observation, and they organise the paper.
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep0.15em
\item \emph{One frozen embedder is one designable lens of association.} The
corpus you train on is the lens you get. So the instrument is manufacturable. A
``legal-risk'' lens is a model frozen on legal text; an ``affective-resonance''
lens is one frozen on annotated affect. You are not stuck with off-the-shelf
semantics. You can commission an instrument for any axis of association you can
assemble a corpus for. The lens is designed by choosing what to remember.
\item \emph{$N$ lenses are $N$ independent association-spaces, and the
$\binom{N}{2}$ cross-terms are associations between associations.} The pairwise
term does not measure the object twice. It measures how the object's position in
lens A relates to its position in lens B. That is a structured-analogy operator,
and it can relate two things that share no surface features at all, because the
relation lives in latent, transitive association.
\item \emph{A centroid plus a threshold is a label.} Name a region of an
association-space (a constellation centroid), gate membership with $G_\tau$, and
continuous geometry becomes a discrete, countable, auditable label. That is
meaning compression in one line: association-geometry turned into a
machine-usable supervisory signal.
\end{enumerate}
Section~\ref{sec:reframe} develops the reframe and its consequences in full; the
rest of the paper formalises and measures them.

\subsection{The three constructs and the contribution}
Derived Data Abundance (DDA) is the principle that a fixed corpus of $n$ inputs,
projected through a panel of $N$ approximately-independent frozen instruments,
yields $n\cdot(N+\binom{N}{2}+1)$ structured supervisory signals at the dataset
level for a code substrate, which works out to as many as 92 per input at $N=13$
once a chunk-foundationality scalar is included. Meaning compression is the ratio
view of that same fact, and we propose it as a fourth taxonomic entry alongside
bit-, weight-, and activation-compression. Teleological Constellation Training
(TCT) is the three-phase method that builds a frozen multi-modal centroid set
$C_P$, trains a generator against it with a geometric-proximity loss, and
enforces a per-output cosine guard $G_\tau$ at inference. The same frozen panel
plays three roles in TCT (retriever, labeller, runtime guard) with no parameter
updates.

So the contribution is one architectural move, three consequences, and one
reframe. The move is to use a single frozen embedder panel as retriever,
labeller, and runtime guard at once, over identical parameter instances. The
consequences are a counting identity ($|D'| = n\cdot(N+\binom{N}{2}+1)$, up to 92
at $N=13$), a constructive sample-complexity bound
($\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{TCT}}(P)=O(M\cdot k)$), and a per-output verifiability
property that scalar-reward alignment does not provide. The reframe is that all
three are best read as a calculus of association over manufacturable measurement
instruments. That reading is what makes the method general across video, code,
and civic matching, and what ties it to the information-physics tradition in
which information is a physical, countable substrate.

We make no claim to ``calculate all of reality.'' The claim is narrower and, we
think, defensible: a frozen embedder panel used as retriever, labeller, and
runtime guard admits a counting identity with a constructive upper bound, a
sample-complexity theorem with measured operating points, and a per-output
verifiability property. All of it is bounded by the associations present in the
chosen corpora, and all of it is auditable.

\section{The Reframe: Frozen Embedders as a Calculus of Association}
\label{sec:reframe}
This section is the conceptual heart of the paper. It states what the later
sections formalise.

\subsection{An embedder is a frozen crystallisation of its corpus's associations}
Let $\phi$ be trained on corpus $D_\phi$ and then frozen
($\partial\phi/\partial\theta = 0$). The learned geometry of $\phi$ encodes the
relational statistics of $D_\phi$: which things co-occur, entail, resemble,
precede, or contrast with which, at whatever level of abstraction the training
objective rewarded. Freezing converts a learner into an instrument, a fixed
function whose output coordinates report a new input's position relative to the
associational structure $\phi$ memorised. Because the instrument is deterministic
and frozen, every later measurement is reproducible and auditable. That is the
precondition for treating $\phi(x)$ as a supervisory signal rather than a
transient activation.

\subsection{The lens is manufacturable}
Because $D_\phi$ determines the lens, the lens is a design choice. This is the
most under-exploited fact in representation learning. Practitioners treat
embedders as fixed commodities, but they are commissionable measuring devices.
Want an instrument that measures ``narrative shape''? Train a classifier-embedding
on story-structure annotations and freeze it (Polis slot S5). Want to separate
``causal want'' from ``causal give''? Train an asymmetric causal embedder on
contribution statements and freeze the cause and effect halves (Polis S3/S4).
Want ``code foundationality''? Compute a PageRank over a frozen dependency graph
(Context Graph $\psi_{\mathrm{found}}$). Assembled this way, the panel is not a
model. It is a library of instruments, built per problem.

\subsection{$N$ lenses and the $\binom{N}{2}$ cross-terms: associations between
associations}
Project an object through $N$ instruments and you get $N$ coordinate vectors, one
per association-space. The pairwise interaction features
$\rho_{jk}(\phi_j(x),\phi_k(x))$ (a normalised cosine, a pairwise
mutual-information estimate, or a concatenation through a frozen adapter) measure
how an object's position in space $j$ relates to its position in space $k$. That
is the structured-analogy operator, and it surfaces relations no single lens can
see. The same operator underlies code-chunk and failure-mode, patch and
nearest-precedent, person and gathering-format, voice-sample and identity: one
mechanism across every domain. Its cardinality is $\binom{N}{2}$, which is why
adding one instrument multiplies value against every existing instrument, and
also why redundancy has to be policed (\S\ref{sec:reframe-honest},
\S\ref{sec:polis}).

\subsection{Centroid plus threshold is a label (meaning compression)}
A frozen centroid $c_m = \overline{\phi_m(R_m)}/\lVert\cdot\rVert$ names a region
of association-space $m$, the region occupied by a reference set $R_m$ (a subject,
a style, an outcome class). The predicate $\ind[\cos(\phi_m(x),c_m)\ge\tau_m]$
turns continuous geometry into a discrete, countable, auditable label: does $x$
belong to the association-region of $R_m$? Stack such labels across the panel and
a fixed, unlabelled corpus becomes a dense, structured supervisory dataset; that
is the static side of meaning compression. Gate generation against the same
labels and alignment becomes checkable geometry; that is the dynamic side.

\subsection{What this makes possible}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.12em
\item A designable measurement basis for meaning. You build the exact
association-axes a problem needs by choosing corpora and freezing, and each new
axis earns its place only by adding differentiated information
(\S\ref{sec:polis}).
\item Structured supervision from fixed, unlabelled data at $N(N-1)/2$ density,
without generating anything, which places it outside the model-collapse recursion
(\S\ref{sec:collapse-scope}).
\item Cross-domain bridging and analogy at scale, through the transitive
cross-terms (\S\ref{sec:reframe}).
\item Per-output, model-free verification. Frozen and deterministic means
membership is reproducible and auditable, so alignment becomes geometry you can
check rather than a scalar you trust (\S\ref{sec:tct}).
\item Identity-locked, controllable generation. Pin a generator to a named region
and it cannot drift out without failing the gate, whether the target is a voice,
a style, a face, or a persona.
\item Field mapping. Project a domain's corpus through the panel and you get a
labelled association-graph of that field (Context Graph's
chunk--skill--ability graph; Polis's 21-axis citizen manifold).
\item Composability and transfer. Train once, reuse forever. The marginal cost of
a new axis is one frozen forward pass and its marginal value is its new bits,
which is a different economics from per-task retraining.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The honest boundary (what keeps the claim defensible)}
\label{sec:reframe-honest}
Stated carefully, the claim survives a metrologist; overstated, it collapses into
``I calculate all of reality.'' Three guardrails keep it on the right side.
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.12em
\item It computes associations that are present in the training corpora, and
those are measurable, bounded, and auditable. A garbage corpus gives a garbage
lens. Spurious associations are a real failure mode, which is why Polis runs a
quarterly redundancy and retirement audit (\S\ref{sec:polis}) and keeps a
confounder and proxy-risk register.
\item The $\binom{N}{2}$ abundance is a constructive upper bound, not a floor. Two
lenses that secretly encode the same associations add almost no bits. The whole
differentiation contract exists because the honest version of ``associations
between anything'' is ``associations between anything, minus the redundant ones,
measured in bits against a real outcome.''
\item Cross-lens comparison stays structured. We use scalar relationships, mutual
information, and agreement, never dim-wise flattening. The associations live
between the spaces, and the panel is never collapsed into a single vector.
\end{itemize}

\section{Related Work (condensed)}
\textbf{Data wall and collapse.} \citet{villalobos2022data} and
\citet{longpre2024consent} frame the stock-and-accessibility problem.
\citet{shumailov2024collapse} (\emph{Nature}), \citet{dohmatob2024tale}, and
\citet{alemohammad2023mad} establish irreversible drift under the recursion
$D_{t+1}\ni\tilde x\sim p_{\theta_t}$. The two cited mitigations,
\citet{gerstgrasser2024inevitable} (accumulation) and \citet{feng2024beyond}
(verification), both stay inside that recursion. DDA's signals are frozen
measurements of real inputs rather than generator samples, so the recursion
precondition simply does not hold (\S\ref{sec:collapse-scope}).

\textbf{Compression as intelligence.} \citet{deletang2024compression} run LLMs as
general compressors, and \citet{huang2024compression} report a correlation of
about $0.95$ between compression ratio and capability across more than thirty
LLMs. The standard taxonomy of bit-, weight-, and activation-compression measures
economy of output relative to a same-modality input. None of it measures
labelled-signal yield per input. We propose meaning compression as a fourth axis
(\S\ref{sec:mc-fourth}), an extension of this line rather than a contradiction of
it.

\textbf{Multi-embedder retrieval.} SigLIP \citep{zhai2023siglip}, Nomic Embed
\citep{nussbaum2024nomic}, WavLM \citep{chen2022wavlm}, ColBERTv2
\citep{santhanam2022colbertv2}, SPLADE \citep{formal2021splade}, and KEPLER
\citep{wang2021kepler}, with HNSW \citep{malkov2018hnsw} and RRF
\citep{cormack2009rrf}, are the standard parts. That literature uses $N$ embedders
as retrieval instruments, scored by NDCG, Recall@k, or EER. None uses the same
$N$ as a labeller over a fixed corpus and a per-output runtime guard at the same
time. The role-flip over identical frozen instances is the novelty relative to
this pool.

\textbf{Identity-locked generation and alignment.} Zero-shot voice cloning (VALL-E
2 at about $0.881$ WavLM-SV \citep{chen2024valle2}; NaturalSpeech 3 at about
$0.891$ \citep{ju2024naturalspeech3}; the field clusters around 0.86 to 0.89) uses
single-prompt conditioning, not a frozen multi-clip centroid with a per-output
cosine gate. Scalar-reward alignment (InstructGPT \citep{ouyang2022instructgpt},
DPO \citep{rafailov2023dpo}, Constitutional AI \citep{bai2022constitutional})
optimises an expectation under a learned scalar and ships no per-output
frozen-threshold predicate. The contrast with TCT is structural rather than a
benchmark contest (\S\ref{sec:tct-vs-scalar}).

\section{DDA and Meaning Compression}

\subsection{The DDA counting identity}
Let $D=\{x_i\}_{i=1}^n$ be a fixed corpus and $\Phi=\{\phi_m\}_{m=1}^N$ a panel of
frozen, approximately-independent instruments. For pairs $j<k$ let $\rho_{jk}$ be
a cross-embedder interaction feature, and for code chunks let
$\psi_{\mathrm{found}}(x)\in[0,1]$ be the PageRank-derived foundationality scalar
from the frozen dependency graph. The derived dataset materialises every
per-embedder projection, every pairwise interaction, and the foundationality
scalar:
\begin{equation}
|D'| = n\cdot\Bigl(N + \tbinom{N}{2} + 1\Bigr),\tag{1}
\end{equation}
which decomposes into $nN$ per-embedder vectors, $n\binom{N}{2}$ pairwise
interaction features, and $n$ graph-foundationality scalars. At $N=13$ the
per-input yield is $13+78+1 = \mathbf{92}$; at $N=7$ (no code graph) it is
$7+21 = \mathbf{28}$; at $N=21$ (no code graph) it is $21+210 = \mathbf{231}$.

Equation (1) is a structural upper bound, not an information-theoretic floor. The
$nN$ term is tight as long as the instruments produce non-constant outputs. The
$\binom{N}{2}$ term carries real information only under approximate orthogonality.
Two instruments that collapse to a linear reparameterisation of each other add
almost no bits, and (1) becomes strict. That is why we write ``up to''
$n\cdot(N+\binom{N}{2}+1)$, and why the pairwise tightness waits on a
mutual-information audit (\S\ref{sec:limits}, EXP-2). A signal here is a scalar or
low-dimensional feature: a deterministic function of a real input or fixed-corpus
graph under frozen instruments, attached to a specific index, usable as input or
target without collecting new data. DDA is therefore not synthetic generation, not
augmentation, and not distillation.

\subsection{Meaning compression as the fourth category}
\label{sec:mc-fourth}
The meaning-compression ratio is
$\mathrm{MC}(\Phi,D) = |D'|/\mathrm{raw}(D) = N+\binom{N}{2}(+1)$ per input.
Conventional compression asks how few bits, parameters, or megabytes a fixed
content can be squeezed into. Meaning compression flips the question: for a fixed
amount of raw data, how many structured supervisory signals can you extract? The
four categories work on genuinely different unit pairs, as Table~\ref{tab:four}
sets out.

\begin{table*}[t]
\centering
\caption{Meaning compression proposed as a fourth category alongside the
conventional three. The categories operate on different input/output unit pairs
and are complementary rather than competing.}
\label{tab:four}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Category} & \textbf{Input unit} & \textbf{Output unit} & \textbf{Canonical techniques}\\
\midrule
Bit        & bits of raw data        & bits of compressed data       & gzip, FLAC, PNG, arithmetic coding\\
Weight     & trainable parameters    & accuracy at those parameters  & GPTQ, SparseGPT, distillation\\
Activation & peak runtime memory     & quality at that memory        & TurboQuant, KV-cache quantisation\\
\textbf{Meaning} & \textbf{one raw input} & \textbf{structured labelled signals} & \textbf{DDA (this paper)}\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table*}

The four are complementary axes, and a single pipeline can realise all of them.
We do not claim meaning compression supersedes the others. Whether reviewers
accept the taxonomic distinction is a scholarly judgement we leave open.

\subsection{Foundationality-graded compression and compression progress}
Two refinements carry over from the production code substrate. The first is
foundationality-graded compression: aggressiveness scales inversely with a
chunk's PageRank foundationality $s_i$, with
$\alpha_{\mathrm{compress}}(x_i)=\mathrm{clip}(1-0.75\,s_i,0.25,1.0)$. Foundational
chunks are sampled $(1+\lambda_{\mathrm{found}}s_i)$ and Fisher-protected
$(1+\lambda_{\mathrm{fisher}}s_i)$ more aggressively, yet compressed less. The
second is compression progress,
$CP_\Phi(t) = H_\Phi(Y\mid X,a;\theta_{t-1}) - H_\Phi(Y\mid X,a;\theta_t)$, the
per-step reduction in bits of the conditional description length of a frozen
target panel under a predictor. It is the Schmidhuberian dynamic dual of meaning
compression's static substrate-density claim. This metric belongs to a
code-verification deployment that is still earlier-stage, so we record it for
completeness, not as a measured result.

\subsection{Scope of the collapse-silent claim}
\label{sec:collapse-scope}
Collapse needs a generator-in-loop recursion
$D_{t+1}\ni\tilde x\sim p_{\theta_t}$. DDA never instantiates one. Each
$\phi_m(x)\in D'$ depends only on a real $x\in D$ and on frozen parameters, so
$D'$ is a decomposition of $D$ rather than a sample from a generator. The
precondition fails, and the cited theorems say nothing about this configuration.
This is a scope argument, not an immunity theorem. We do not claim a new
independence-from-collapse result, and we hold two boundary cases explicitly out
of scope: inference-time best-of-$K$ selection, which updates no parameter, and
embedder-mediated data curation, which has a different feedback structure.

\subsection{The three-role composition}
The same frozen $\Phi$ plays three roles at once over identical parameter
instances, with no parameter update between or during them. As retriever it ranks
stored content by cosine. As labeller it materialises the signals of (1). As
runtime guard it evaluates a candidate against a frozen centroid (\S\ref{sec:tct}).
Relative to the locked reference set, no cited paper composes all three roles
together over identical frozen instances.

\section{Three Production Systems}
Three antecedent systems instantiate the labeller role at increasing panel size.
All counts are system-at-date figures taken from the working tree, so the
relationship is a constructive witness, not a controlled experimental platform.

\subsection{ClipCannon: video, $N=7$, up to 28 signals/input}
ClipCannon is a ${\approx}67{,}585$-line Python package that runs a 23-stage
analysis DAG over source video. It extracts seven modalities: visual
(SigLIP-SO400M, 1{,}152-D), semantic (nomic-embed-text-v1.5, 768-D), emotion
(wav2vec2-large, 1{,}024-D), speaker (WavLM-large, 512-D), prosody (custom 12-D),
sentiment (MiniLM-L6-v2, 384-D), and voice identity (ECAPA-TDNN, 192-D). That is
4{,}044 dimensions across seven modalities, exposed as 58 MCP tools, with a
SHA-256 tamper-evident provenance chain. At $N=7$ it yields up to 28
signals/input. It is the labeller for Cases~2 and~3, and the curation substrate
that turns one 16-minute interview into 2{,}362 training clips.

\subsection{Context Graph: text and code, $N=13$, up to 92 signals/input}
Context Graph is a ${\approx}370{,}000$-line Rust workspace that embeds every
stored memory through 13 frozen embedders (E1 to E13). Together they span 11{,}008
dense dimensions, two 30{,}522-term sparse vocabularies (E6 lexical, E13 SPLADE),
and a 128-per-token late-interaction space (E12 ColBERTv2). The remaining slots
are e5-large-v2 semantic (E1), sinusoidal and Fourier temporal (E2 to E4),
asymmetric Nomic causal (E5), Qodo 1{,}536-D code (E7), structural e5 for graph
edges (E8), 10{,}000-bit HDC typo-robust (E9), paraphrase-asymmetric e5-base
(E10), and KEPLER entity geometry (E11). E5, E8, and E10 store dual vectors
(cause/effect; source/target; doc/query). Persistence is 59 RocksDB column
families, and retrieval is weighted RRF over the per-embedder rankings, exposed as
75 MCP tools. At $N=13$ it yields up to 92 signals/input ($13+78+1$). Context
Graph is the backbone of Leapable.ai's knowledge-vault product and the labeller
for Case~1.

\subsection{socialmedia2.com / Polis: civic matching, $N=21$, and the
differentiation contract}
\label{sec:polis}
The most information-theoretically explicit instantiation is the civic-matching
system behind socialmedia2.com (codename Polis), which matches citizens into
durable real-world gatherings instead of optimising engagement. Its constellation
is a 21-slot slate across 11 orthogonal axes plus a hard-constraint vector,
roughly 35{,}800 dimensions per citizen, and it is never flattened. The axes and a
few representative slots: semantic content (S1 \texttt{sem-self}, S2
\texttt{lex-vocab} SPLADE), causal want and give (S3/S4 asymmetric Nomic-causal),
narrative form (S5 \texttt{story-shape}, S6 \texttt{hero-arc}), cognitive mode (S7
\texttt{reason-mode}, S8 \texttt{abstract-concrete}), moral foundations (S9
\texttt{mfq-vec}), ideological bridging (S10 \texttt{bridge-vec}), somatic and
prosodic (S11 to S13 voice tempo, affect, energy), temporal rhythm (S14/S15
Fourier day and week), aesthetic field (S16 SigLIP-2 on curated images, S17
stylometry), network position (S18 TransE-class, S19 Burt structural-hole), and
relational dynamics (S20 conflict-style, S21 care-mode). At $N=21$ the DDA
identity gives up to 231 signals/input ($21+210$).

Polis is where the honest boundary of \S\ref{sec:reframe-honest} hardens into an
enforced engineering contract. A slate $S$ is differentiated when:
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.12em
\item \textbf{(D1) Distinctness.}
$\forall i\neq j:\ \mathrm{corr}_+(E_i(X),E_j(X))\le 0.6$ on a calibration set
$|X|\ge 500$.
\item \textbf{(D2) Marginal information.}
$\forall i:\ H(y\mid E_1..E_{i-1}) - H(y\mid E_1..E_i)\ \ge\ 0.05$ bits, where $y$
is the binary 30-day in-person tie-formation outcome.
\item \textbf{(D3) Frozen.}
$\forall i,\ \forall\text{ phase}\in\{\text{match, train, eval, serve}\}:\
\partial E_i/\partial\theta_i=0$.
\end{itemize}
The principle behind the three conditions is that a panel's value is
$\sum_i\Delta H_i = \sum_i I(y;E_i\mid E_1..E_{i-1})$, not $\sum_i H(E_i)$. A
redundant slot, with $\mathrm{corr}\approx1$, contributes $\Delta H_i\approx 0$.
It is noise per token, and it is retired at the quarterly audit that runs over all
$\binom{21}{2}=210$ pairs. This is the calculus of association turned into a build
gate: instruments are admitted in bits against a real outcome, not by raw
dimensionality. On status, we stay honest. The slate registry, the 21-slot
constellation assembly, slot-shape validation, the semantic probe suite, and the
pairwise-redundancy report are implemented and operational; a five-answer intake,
for instance, produces a complete 21-slot constellation, and
\texttt{assemble\_constellation} succeeds only when all 21 slots are present. What
is still pending is the real ${\ge}500$-citizen calibration corpus and the 30-day
outcome validation that would turn D2 from a contract into a measured pass. The
system is pre-launch, and its mechanical \texttt{BUILD\_DONE} predicate is
deliberately false until that real evidence exists.

\subsection{The three systems on one curve}
Plot signals-per-input, $N+\binom{N}{2}(+1)$, against $N$ and the three systems
land on a single quadratic: ClipCannon at $N=7\to28$, Context Graph at
$N=13\to92$, Polis at $N=21\to231$. Each added instrument multiplies associations
against all the existing ones. That is the engine of meaning compression, and the
differentiation contract is what keeps the growth signal rather than noise.

\section{Teleological Constellation Training}
\label{sec:tct}

\subsection{The constellation $C_P$}
For a subject $P$ and a modality set $\mathcal{M}=\{m_1,..,m_M\}$, with a reference
set $R_m$ and a frozen embedder $\phi_m$ per modality, the constellation is
$C_P=\{(m,c_m):m\in\mathcal{M}\}$. Each $c_m$ is a frozen unit-norm centroid,
double-normalised: per-sample L2, then the mean, then L2 again, with samples of
norm below $10^{-6}$ excluded and a leave-one-out cosine below $0.85$ flagged as
degenerate. The constellation is the single object the method is defined against.
DDA produces the projections it is built from, Phase~2 targets it, and Phase~3
verifies it.

\subsection{Phase 2: constellation-conditioned training}
The training objective is
$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{TCT}}(\theta) = \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{gen}} +
\sum_m\lambda_m(1-\cos(\phi_m(\hat y),c_m))$. Gradients flow through $\hat y$ into
$\theta$, but never into $\phi_m$ or $c_m$: the embedders stay frozen, LoRA sits on
the generator only, and centroid hashes are checked before and after. Cosine is
computed in fp32 against a bf16 generator forward, which removes
threshold-proximity nondeterminism. An over-high $\lambda_m$ collapses the
generator onto the constellation independent of conditioning, with $\cos\to1.0$
while $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{gen}}$ stalls; the mitigation is to halve $\lambda_m$.
When the backbone is inference-only, as in Case~3, Phase~2 reduces to best-of-$K$
selection: draw $K$ candidates, score $s_m^{(k)}=\cos(\phi_m(\hat y^{(k)}),c_m)$,
and return $\arg\max_k\sum_m w_m s_m^{(k)}$.

\subsection{Phase 3: the runtime guard $G_\tau$}
\begin{equation}
G_\tau(\hat y;C_P) = \ind\bigl[\forall m\in\mathcal{M}:\
\cos(\phi_m(\hat y),c_m)\ge\tau_m\bigr].\tag{5}
\end{equation}
The guard is per-output, costs $O(M\cdot d_{\max})$, uses no auxiliary learned
model, and is deterministic given the tensors. Each $\tau_m$ is calibrated before
deployment to a same-session-band floor: the 10th percentile of authentic-sample
cosines on a disjoint calibration set. Tuning $\tau$ on the evaluation set would
invalidate the guard. Every returned output carries a cosine trace, an embedder
weight hash, a centroid hash, the threshold set, the decision, and the attempt
count, which together form the audit record that makes the guard verifiable after
the fact. What is verified is per-output verifiability relative to the chosen
centroid panel: that $\hat y$ satisfies the cosine predicate against frozen $C_P$
under $\Phi$. It is not a claim that $\hat y$ is correct under some external
criterion the panel cannot see.

\subsection{Two theorems}
\textbf{Theorem 1 (constructive sample complexity).} Under the Phase~1/2/3
structure and Axioms~1 to~3, the subject-specific sample footprint is
$\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{TCT}}(P)=O(\sum_m n_m)=O(M\cdot k)$, independent of $|D|$.
The bound is constructive: any pipeline with exactly those phases satisfies it by
definition. Its empirical content, that $n_m$ in the range $[10,10^2]$ suffices
for a stable centroid, rests on the measured operating points of
\S\ref{sec:cases}.

\textbf{Theorem 2 (per-output verifiability).} $G_\tau$ is a deterministic
$O(M\cdot d_{\max})$ geometric test that admits no auxiliary learned model, a
property scalar-reward alignment does not furnish in the same technical sense
(\S\ref{sec:tct-vs-scalar}).

\section{Case Studies}
\label{sec:cases}
Every number below is tagged MEASURED or PROJECTED. Measured numbers carry their
date and pipeline version; projected numbers are flagged clause by clause.

\subsection{Case 1: Shakespeare stylistic LoRA (MEASURED end-to-end 2026-04-17)}
The target was an open-weight LLM that answers any modern question in Shakespearean
style with two hard properties: it never refuses a topic, and it never drops the
style under prompt injection. The base was Gemma-4-E4B-it (NF4 at inference), with
a LoRA of rank 256 and $\alpha=512$ across 258 modules. The corpus was the
Shakespeare canon projected through Context Graph's 13-embedder panel as DDA
labels: 2{,}698 memories furnishing roughly 249{,}000 supervisory signals (13
topic-profile scores plus 78 pairwise cross-correlations per memory, 22{,}152 typed
edges, and constellation conditioning), distilled down to a forensically-cleaned
7{,}925 SFT examples and 11{,}724 DPO pairs scored against a 9-embedder
STYLE\_CENTROID (E2, E3, E5, and E11 were left out as orthogonal to style).

The measured results: SFT loss fell to 1.12 (from about 12) at 98.9\% token
accuracy over 765 steps (164.7 min); DPO loss fell to 0.47 (from 1.75) at 98.0\%
rewards-accuracy with \texttt{rewards/margins} of 43.62 (282.4 min); about 7.5
hours total on one RTX 5090. On held-out behavioural probes the model wrote love
poetry, explained quantum entanglement with reference to Bell's theorem in verse,
refused an admin-override prompt-injection attempt while staying in character,
produced a Go binary search with Shakespearean code comments, and, when challenged
in Spanish, answered in an archaic register (more on that below). The weights are
published at \path{huggingface.co/cabdru/shakespeare-lora-gemma4}.

There was also a forensic finding, and it is where multi-embedder guarding earned
its keep. An earlier run passed its integrity checks yet echoed each prompt in
modern English before turning Shakespearean. Single-embedder E1 similarity missed
the problem completely. The 13-embedder audit caught it: E4 (position-ordering)
and E12 (token-level MaxSim) flagged an opening-position style anomaly that was
invisible to overall-document similarity. The root cause was mundane. 67\%
(5{,}954 of 8{,}857) of the original SFT examples began with inherited play-script
headers (``SCENE III. \dots\ Enter Cassius \dots''). The fix was data preparation.
The lesson is that single-embedder evaluation misses opening-position pathologies
that are highly salient to a human reader, which is the standing argument for
multi-embedder guarding.

The emergent finding is measured but single-instance. A Spanish prompt demanding a
Spanish answer produced Siglo de Oro Spanish, in the Cervantes, Lope, and
Calder\'on register of roughly 1580 to 1630, rather than modern Spanish. It used
archaic forms (\emph{vos}, \emph{vuestro}, \emph{ojal\'as}, \emph{obsequie}),
archaic vocabulary (\emph{ocaso}, \emph{reino celestial}, \emph{sacerdotes y
sabios}, \emph{perdici\'on}), and Shakespearean rhetoric (cascading polysyndeton,
apostrophe to celestial bodies, prophetic portents). The base model without the
LoRA does not do this. Read through \S\ref{sec:reframe}, the explanation is that
the LoRA learned ``archaic literary register'' as a cross-signal fingerprint
across language-agnostic association components, namely E9 (rhythm and cadence),
E7 (periodic structure), E10 (surface-form underdetermines meaning), and E1
(cosmic-order, fate, and honour semantic field), and forcing Spanish retrieved the
nearest manifestation of that fingerprint. It is single-language, single-register,
and small-sample. A systematic cross-language matrix is named future work, and we
do not rule out alternative accounts such as latent base-model competence.

\subsection{Case 2: identity-locked talking-head ``Santa'' (PROJECTED end-to-end;
forensic integrity complete)}
The target was an audio-driven talking-head that reproduces one subject visually
and behaviourally, with an eight-state emotional constellation and per-frame
identity verification, built from a 16-minute 15-second 2560$\times$1440/60fps
interview. Over five days we evaluated 15 renderer architectures; EchoMimicV3 LoRA
gave the best identity lock. The constellation is a seven-modality ClipCannon panel
taken after diarisation: visual 1{,}725$\times$1{,}152-D, semantic 200$\times$768-D,
emotion 343$\times$1{,}024-D, speaker 300$\times$512-D, prosody 188$\times$12-D,
sentiment 202$\times$384-D, and voice 188$\times$192-D, with projected thresholds of
0.70 visual, 0.60 emotion, 0.80 speaker, and 0.85 voice.

What is measured or complete: the meeting-integration pipeline (Chrome-extension
detector, Xvfb bot, Playwright Google-Meet client, Insertable-Streams injection);
the audio stack (Whisper plus Gemma reasoning at about 509 ms, with same-subject
voice-clone SECS above 0.95); a constellation expression system (40 expression
groups to 34 skills to 8 constellations) whose \texttt{ConstellationGuard}
re-embeds each frame through SigLIP and validates cosine 1.0 against the reference
while rejecting stranger faces below 0.5; a YOLOv5-Face pipeline over 24{,}397
frames at about 90\% detection; and a sub-5-ms PhysicsFaceEngine. The forensic work
is load-bearing. LoRA versions 1 through 5 trained with clean loss yet produced
dead lip-sync, because the training target convention was inverted
(\texttt{target = latent $-$ noise} where the scheduler expects
\texttt{target = noise $-$ latent}). Loss convergence did not verify trajectory
correctness; only the constellation-side audit surfaced the bug. Five companion
fixes followed: a frame-rate mismatch, a mouth-box-on-nose error, a name-shadowing
\texttt{q\_audio} dead-gradient typo, a 22 GB feature RAM leak, and a
uniform-instead-of-weighted viseme curriculum. What is still pending is the
corrected-sign end-to-end identity-cosine table and the lip-sync LSE-D. Every
quantitative row in this subsection is projected, and a later version will
re-verify it.

\subsection{Case 3: voice cloning, 50-clip centroid (MEASURED 2026-04-16)}
This is the quantitative anchor. The protocol was Qwen3-TTS (inference-only) with
Full In-Context Learning, a 50-clip WavLM-base-plus-sv centroid (the top 50 of a
489-candidate pool), and WavLM-scored best-of-12 at $T=0.3$, with $\tau=0.95$
recorded for audit. It was measured on $N=10$ held-out English sentences, one
speaker, encoder-matched (the guard and the evaluator are both
WavLM-base-plus-sv), under pipeline \texttt{qwen3-tts-foricl-v1}:
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.1em
\item Mean WavLM SECS 0.961; max 0.975; min 0.914; 9 of 10 at or above 0.95.
\item DNSMOS P.808: clone 3.93 versus real 3.93 ($N=14$); UTMOS: clone 3.012
versus real 2.997.
\item A within-WavLM-family 95\% bootstrap CI on the mean of [0.951, 0.971] (10k
resamples, seed 20260416; retrospective, not power-based).
\item $T=0.3$ was the sweep optimum (sweep mean 0.932, max 0.963; the headline run
was 0.961 / 0.975).
\item For field reference: VALL-E 2 at about $0.881$ \citep{chen2024valle2} and
NaturalSpeech 3 at about $0.891$ \citep{ju2024naturalspeech3}, with the field
clustering around 0.86 to 0.89.
\end{itemize}

One scope disclosure is mandatory. The 0.961 is encoder-matched: the selector and
the scorer are the same WavLM family, so the figure demonstrates that the guard
behaves as specified, but it is not cross-encoder identity verification. A survivor
of selection under $\phi$ scores high on $\phi$ by construction. The cross-encoder
triangulation, an ECAPA-TDNN \citep{desplanques2020ecapa} re-score of the ten
argmax candidates, is instrument-ready and pending (\S\ref{sec:limits}, EXP-1).
Read through \S2.4, the 0.961 is the measured endpoint of generation collapsing
into the named association-region of the speaker centroid, a tight, low-entropy
cluster around identity.

\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Interpreting the measured anchors}
The Case~3 anchor supports a narrow reading. Under the fixed pipeline,
post-selection mean WavLM SECS is 0.961, and 9 of 10 English sentences clear 0.95
at $M=1$ for one speaker, with naturalness within evaluator noise of the
reference. It does not support cross-encoder triangulation,
multi-speaker or multi-language generalisation, or the $M>1$ conjunction, which is
the territory Case~2 would land and Case~2 is projected. Case~1 is a second
measured anchor at $M=1$ on text style (public weights, prompt-injection-robust)
with an emergent out-of-distribution generalisation observation.

\subsection{The DDA bound is an upper bound, not a floor}
The $\binom{N}{2}$ term is the vulnerable part. Heavy redundancy between any pair,
say E1 semantic against E10 paraphrase, both dense-text, contracts the effective
count below 92. Qualitative blind-spot evidence, where sparse E6 and E13 recall
things E1 misses, E9 HDC handles typos, and E7 ranks code, shows the effective
count strictly exceeds $N$. The tight pairwise value still waits on the
mutual-information audit (EXP-2). The Polis D1/D2 contract is the same insight made
operational, and it is the reason Polis is the cleanest demonstration that
``associations between anything'' has to be read as ``the differentiated
associations, measured in bits.''

\subsection{TCT versus scalar-reward alignment: a different property}
\label{sec:tct-vs-scalar}
TCT aims at a frozen multi-modal constellation rather than a learned scalar. Its
instrument is a frozen panel rather than a learned reward model, its metric is
per-modality cosine, and its runtime posture is a per-output guard. We do not
claim TCT beats RLHF, DPO, or Constitutional AI on preference benchmarks; the
comparison is structural. Per-output verifiability relative to a chosen panel is
something scalar-reward alignment cannot furnish even in principle, because it
optimises an expectation and ships no frozen threshold against which each output is
accepted or rejected at inference without extra learned machinery. Two scope
qualifications apply: the guard is panel-relative, so cross-panel transport is
open, and it is predicate-level, not identity-level under any external criterion.

\subsection{Honest failure modes are part of the method}
Both load-bearing bugs, Case~2's inverted flow-matching sign and Case~1's 67\%
header echo, produced pipelines that passed superficial health checks and would
have shipped under single-embedder evaluation. The multi-embedder, multi-role
composition is load-bearing at diagnosis time, not only at training time. Forensic
audit with the frozen panel is a deployment requirement, and the three-role
composition is where that audit happens.

\subsection{Implications}
For identity-locked generation, a per-output verifiable predicate against a frozen
centroid panel is instrument-ready and measurable, with encoder-matched scope on
Case~3 and multi-modal results pending on Case~2. For scaling-law policy, there is
a genuine third path: decompose a fixed corpus through $N$ frozen instruments for
up to $n\cdot(N+\binom{N}{2})$ signals, with no generation and no recursion, which
extends rather than contradicts the compression-as-intelligence frame. For data
strategy, the fixed corpus an organisation already holds admits an upper-bounded
multiplication of structured signals per input, with the tight count pending the
MI audit.

\section{Limitations and Open Problems}
\label{sec:limits}
\textbf{L1, encoder-matched Case 3.} 0.961 is within-WavLM-family agreement, not
cross-encoder verification, and the CI is retrospective. Remediation is EXP-1, the
ECAPA-TDNN re-score.\\
\textbf{L2, one speaker, one language.} No multi-speaker or multi-language
generalisation is supported; the scope tag is the boundary.\\
\textbf{L3, Case 2 projected, Case 1 per-embedder held-out pending.} The
constructive theorems and the DDA identity do not depend on either.\\
\textbf{L4, embedder independence is empirical, not proven.} Axiom~2 is an
operational hedge, and the $\binom{N}{2}$ term needs EXP-2.\\
\textbf{L5, $\tau$ calibration is per-panel and per-application.} These are
observed values, not universal constants, and cross-subject transport is open.\\
\textbf{L6, constellation degeneration under non-diverse references.} Narrow
reference pools collapse the centroid; characterisation is pending.\\
\textbf{L7, inference cost.} Full ICL plus best-of-12 is inference-expensive, and
no distilled guard is proposed here.\\
\textbf{L8, competing interests.} The author operates Leapable.ai and
socialmedia2.com, and Context Graph, ClipCannon, and Polis are production systems
from that practice. No vendor-funded benchmark, paid comparison, or
commercial-performance claim is reported. Every projected number is flagged and
every softening disclosed.

\medskip
\noindent Three experiments would most directly tighten or kill the claims. EXP-1
is an ECAPA-TDNN cross-encoder re-score of the Case~3 argmax candidates; it
promotes the headline to cross-encoder identity ranking if the mean is at least
$0.85$ with Pearson at least $0.6$, and otherwise the within-family scope holds.
EXP-2 is a pairwise mutual-information audit across the 13-embedder panel and the
21-slot Polis slate on 100 to 1{,}000 inputs; it would replace the pairwise part of
``up to 92 / up to 231'' with a measured $N_{\mathrm{eff}}$, and would falsify the
counting argument if the pairwise term contracts heavily. EXP-3 is a four-point
sweep of $n_m\in\{10,25,50,100\}$ on Case~3, which would promote the one-row
sample-complexity anchor to a partial curve. Beyond those, Polis's real
${\ge}500$-citizen D2 calibration against 30-day tie outcomes would turn its
differentiation contract from enforced-by-construction into measured-in-bits.

\section{Conclusion}
A frozen embedder is a reusable instrument that has memorised the
association-structure of its training data, and the lens is designed by choosing
what to remember. Stack a differentiated panel of such instruments and you can
measure any object's position across many association-spaces at once, quantify the
relations between those positions (the $\binom{N}{2}$ cross-terms), and freeze
named regions of the geometry into auditable labels. Fixed, unlabelled data
becomes a machine-legible map of how things relate. That is what we mean by
meaning compression, the calculus of association, proposed as a fourth compression
category and instantiated across video ($N=7$), code ($N=13$), and civic matching
($N=21$). We measured it where it ran: a voice reproduced at 0.961
speaker-similarity (encoder-matched), a style model with public weights that holds
character under prompt injection, and a per-output geometric guard with
deterministic provenance. The claims stay bounded by the associations present in
the chosen corpora, not ``all of reality,'' and the abundance is a constructive
upper bound pending a mutual-information audit. Three named experiments would
tighten or kill each load-bearing claim. The contribution is not a new primitive
but a composition of existing ones read in a new light: information made into a
physical, reusable, designable measurement basis for meaning.
